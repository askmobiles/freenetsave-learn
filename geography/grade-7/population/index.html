Learn what you need to know

There are about 8 billion people on Earth. The population grew slowly for thousands of years, then very fast after about 1800 thanks to better food, medicine and clean water.

Population distribution is where people live. Population density is people per km². Most people live where land is flat, climate is mild and there is water and food: near coasts and rivers. Few live in very cold, dry or mountainous places.

🇨🇦Canada: about 41 million people, about 4 per km² — but most live within 160 km of the US border
🇧🇩Bangladesh: about 1300 people per km²
🇮🇳India and China: about 1.4 billion people each
🏙️Over half the world now lives in cities

Natural increase = birth rate − death rate. A population pyramid shows the ages and sexes of a population: a wide base means many young people; a narrow base means an aging population.

Density formula: population ÷ area. A country with 10 million people and 100 000 km² has a density of 100 people per km².

Key words

  • population density: the number of people per square kilometre
  • population distribution: how people are spread out across an area
  • birth rate: births per 1000 people per year
  • population pyramid: a graph showing a population by age and sex
