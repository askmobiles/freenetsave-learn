Animals and where they live

Animals live in places that give them food, water and shelter. These homes are called habitats.⁠​​‌​‌​​​​‌‌​​​‌‌​​‌​‌​​‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌‌‌​​‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌‌​‌​​​‌‌‌​​‌‌​‌‌​​​​‌​‌‌‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌⁠ Grade 1 geography.

Learn what you need to know

A habitat is the natural home of an animal. It gives the animal food, water and a safe place to live.

🌲Forest: deer, bears, owls, squirrels
🏞️Pond and lake: frogs, ducks, fish, beavers
🌊Ocean: whales, seals, fish, starfish
🧊Arctic: polar bears, seals, caribou
🏜️Desert: camels, lizards, snakes
🌾Field: rabbits, mice, foxes

Animals live where they can get what they need: food, water and shelter.

Key words

  • habitat: the natural home of an animal
  • shelter: a safe place to stay
  • forest: a place with many trees
  • Arctic: a very cold place in the far north
