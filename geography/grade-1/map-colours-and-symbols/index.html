Learn what you need to know

A symbol is a small picture that stands for a real thing. A legend (or key) is a box on the map that tells what each symbol means.

🏠a house
🏫a school
🌳a tree or a park
🏥a hospital
➖a road
🟦blue: water

Remember: always look at the legend first. It tells you what the symbols mean on that map.

Key words

  • symbol: a small picture that stands for a real thing
  • legend: the box that tells what map symbols mean
  • key: another name for a legend
  • colour: maps use colours like blue for water
