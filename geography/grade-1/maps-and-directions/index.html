Maps and directions

A map is a drawing of a place from above. Use words like left, right, near and far to tell where things are.⁠​​‌​‌​​​​‌‌​​​‌‌​​‌​‌​​‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌‌‌​​‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌‌​‌​​​‌‌‌​​‌‌​‌‌​​​​‌​‌‌‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌⁠ Grade 1 geography.

Learn what you need to know

A map is a picture of a place as if you were a bird looking down from the sky.

⬅️Left
➡️Right
⬆️Up, in front, above
⬇️Down, behind, below
📏Near means close. Far means a long way away.

Try it: "The park is next to the school. The store is far from my house." Position words help us give directions.

Key words

  • map: a drawing of a place seen from above
  • near: close by
  • far: a long way away
  • direction: the way to go
