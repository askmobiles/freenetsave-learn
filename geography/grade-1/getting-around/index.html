We choose how to travel by how far we are going. Walking is good for short trips. A plane is good for very long trips.

Stay safe: stop, look left, look right and look left again before you cross. Use crosswalks. Wear a helmet on a bike.

Key words

  • transportation: ways to move people and things
  • crosswalk: a marked place to cross the street
  • helmet: a hard hat that protects your head
  • traffic light: lights that tell cars and people when to stop and go
