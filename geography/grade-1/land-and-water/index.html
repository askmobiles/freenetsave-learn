Learn what you need to know

Our Earth is covered with land and water. There is more water than land!

⛰️Mountain: very tall, high land
🟫Hill: land that is higher, but not as tall as a mountain
🌾Plain: flat land
🏝️Island: land with water all around it
🌊Ocean: a very big body of salt water
🏞️Lake: water with land all around it
〰️River: water that flows across the land
💧Pond: a small lake

On a map and a globe: water is usually blue. Land is often green or brown.

Key words

  • land: the ground we walk on
  • ocean: a very big body of salt water
  • lake: water with land all around it
  • island: land with water all around it
