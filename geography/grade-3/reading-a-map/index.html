Reading a map

Every good map has a title, a legend, a compass rose and a scale. Learn what each part does.⁠​​‌​‌​​​​‌‌​​​‌‌​​‌​‌​​‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌‌‌​​‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌‌​‌​​​‌‌‌​​‌‌​‌‌​​​​‌​‌‌‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌⁠ Grade 3 geography.

Learn what you need to know

📝Title: tells what the map shows
🔑Legend (key): explains the symbols and colours
🧭Compass rose: shows north, south, east and west
📏Scale: shows how distance on the map compares to real distance
🔢Grid: lines with letters and numbers to help find places, like B3

Scale example: if 1 cm on the map = 1 km in real life, then 3 cm on the map = 3 km.

TLC-S: remember Title, Legend, Compass rose and Scale — the four parts of a good map.

Key words

  • title: tells what the map is about
  • scale: shows how far real places are
  • compass rose: shows the directions on a map
  • grid: lines that make boxes to help find places
