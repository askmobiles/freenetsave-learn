Learn what you need to know

🪨Canadian Shield: most of Ontario; old rock, forests and thousands of lakes
🌾Great Lakes–St. Lawrence Lowlands: the south; flat land and rich soil; most people live here
🌿Hudson Bay Lowlands: the far north; wet, flat land called muskeg, near Hudson Bay
🌊Four Great Lakes touch Ontario: Superior, Huron, Erie and Ontario
🏖️Hudson Bay and James Bay are in the north
〰️Big rivers: the Ottawa River, the St. Lawrence River, the Niagara River

Ontario has over 250,000 lakes! Water covers about one-sixth of the province.

Key words

  • Canadian Shield: a huge area of old rock, forests and lakes
  • lowlands: low, mostly flat land
  • muskeg: wet, spongy ground in the far north
  • region: an area with things in common
