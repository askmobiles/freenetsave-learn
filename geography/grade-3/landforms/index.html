Learn what you need to know

A landform is a natural shape on the Earth's surface. Landforms are made by nature, not by people.

⛰️Mountain: very high land with steep sides (the Rocky Mountains)
🟫Hill: raised land, lower and rounder than a mountain
🏞️Valley: low land between hills or mountains, often with a river
🌾Plain: wide, flat land (the Prairies)
🏔️Plateau: high, flat land
🏝️Island: land with water all around (Prince Edward Island)
🌊Peninsula: land with water on three sides (Nova Scotia)
🏖️Bay: part of a lake or ocean that goes into the land (Hudson Bay)

Island vs peninsula: an island has water on ALL sides; a peninsula has water on THREE sides and is joined to the land.

Key words

  • landform: a natural shape of the land
  • valley: low land between hills or mountains
  • peninsula: land with water on three sides
  • plateau: high, flat land
