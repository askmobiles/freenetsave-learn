City and country communities

Learn what you need to know

🏙️Urban (city): many people close together; tall buildings, buses, subways; Toronto, Ottawa
🏘️Suburban: near a city; houses with yards, plazas and schools
🚜Rural (country): fewer people spread out; farms, forests, small towns

Compare: in a city you might take the subway and live in an apartment. In the country you might ride a school bus a long way and live on a farm.

Cities and farms need each other: farms grow food for cities, and cities make things farms need, like tools and medicine.

Key words

  • urban: a city area with many people
  • rural: a country area with fewer people and more land
  • suburban: an area near a city with houses and yards
  • population: the number of people who live in a place
