Learn what you need to know

Money is not the only way to measure how well people live. Geographers use indicators:

❤️Life expectancy: how long people live on average
👶Infant mortality: babies who die before age 1, per 1000 births
📚Literacy rate: percent of adults who can read and write
🎓Years of schooling
💧Access to clean water and sanitation
🍽️Calories and nutrition

The Human Development Index (HDI) by the United Nations combines health (life expectancy), education (years of schooling) and income (GNI per capita) into one number from 0 to 1. Countries like Switzerland, Norway and Iceland score about 0.96; Canada about 0.94; some countries score below 0.45.

Quality of life also varies within countries: between cities and rural areas, and between groups. In Canada, some First Nations communities have lower life expectancy and lack clean water.

Use several indicators: one number never tells the whole story.

Key words

  • indicator: a measurement used to compare quality of life
  • life expectancy: the average number of years a person is expected to live
  • infant mortality rate: the number of babies per 1000 who die before age 1
  • Human Development Index: a UN measure combining health, education and income
