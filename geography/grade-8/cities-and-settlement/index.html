Learn what you need to know

A settlement is any place where people live, from a hamlet to a megacity. Settlements grow where there are good site and situation factors.

📍Site: the land itself — flat land, fresh water, good soil, protection
🧭Situation: location compared with other places — on a trade route, river mouth, crossroads, harbour

Toronto grew on a natural harbour on Lake Ontario, at the start of trade routes north. Montreal grew where the St. Lawrence rapids stopped ships.

🏘️Hamlet / village / town / city / metropolis
🏙️Megacity: over 10 million people (Tokyo, Delhi, Shanghai, São Paulo, Lagos)
📈Urbanization: in 1950 about 30% of people lived in cities; today over 55%; by 2050 about 68%

Rural areas have few people spread out, often farming. Urban areas are built up and dense. In Canada over 80% of people live in urban areas.

Settlement patterns: linear (along a road or river), clustered (around a centre), or scattered (spread out farms).

Key words

  • settlement: a place where people live
  • site: the physical land a settlement is built on
  • situation: a settlement's location compared with places around it
  • urbanization: the growth of cities as people move from rural areas
