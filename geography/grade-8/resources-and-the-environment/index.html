Resources and the environment

Learn what you need to know

As settlements grow, people need more land, water, energy and materials. This creates environmental impacts.

🚗Air pollution from cars, factories and power plants
💧Water pollution from sewage, farms and industry
🗑️Waste: Canadians make about 700 kg of garbage per person per year
🌳Habitat loss as cities and farms spread
🔥Greenhouse gases causing climate change

Your ecological footprint measures how much land and water is needed to supply what you use and absorb your waste. If everyone lived like the average Canadian, we would need about 4–5 Earths.

Sustainable communities use renewable energy, good public transit, green roofs and parks, water conservation, composting and recycling, and protect natural areas.

Stewardship: taking care of the environment for the future. Many Indigenous teachings describe responsibility to the land for seven generations.

Key words

  • environmental impact: a change to the environment caused by people
  • ecological footprint: the land and water needed to support a person's lifestyle
  • stewardship: responsible care of the environment
  • sustainable community: a community that meets its needs without harming the future
