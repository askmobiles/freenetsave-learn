Learn what you need to know

The world is very unequal. The richest 10% of people own over three-quarters of global wealth, while about 800 million people live in extreme poverty (less than about $3 US a day).

🏚️Causes: colonial history, conflict, debt, unfair trade, poor governance, climate disasters, lack of education and health care
🔁The poverty cycle: poor health and little schooling → low income → poor health…
🌍Effects: hunger, disease, child labour, migration, lack of opportunity

The United Nations' Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) are 17 goals for 2030, including no poverty, zero hunger, quality education, gender equality and clean water.

Responses include foreign aid from governments, work by NGOs (like UNICEF, the Red Cross, Oxfam and Doctors Without Borders), fair trade, debt relief and local community projects.

Your role: learn about issues, make thoughtful buying choices, support reliable organizations and speak up.

Key words

  • extreme poverty: living on less than about $3 US a day
  • poverty cycle: how poverty causes conditions that keep people poor
  • Sustainable Development Goals: 17 UN goals to improve the world by 2030
  • debt relief: cancelling or reducing money a country owes
