Learn what you need to know

Canada is the second-biggest country in the world. It has 10 provinces and 3 territories.

🏛️Our capital city is Ottawa, in Ontario
🗺️We live in the province of Ontario
🏙️Toronto is the biggest city in Canada and the capital of Ontario
🇺🇸Our neighbour to the south is the United States

Where am I? My home → my street → my city or town → Ontario → Canada → North America → Earth.

Key words

  • country: a land with its own government and flag
  • province: a part of Canada, like Ontario
  • territory: a northern part of Canada, like Nunavut
  • capital: the city where the government meets
