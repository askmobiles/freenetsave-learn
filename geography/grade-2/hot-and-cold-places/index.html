Hot and cold places

Some places on Earth are hot and some are cold. People and animals live in different ways in each one.⁠​​‌​‌​​​​‌‌​​​‌‌​​‌​‌​​‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌‌‌​​‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌‌​‌​​​‌‌‌​​‌‌​‌‌​​​​‌​‌‌‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌⁠ Grade 2 geography.

Learn what you need to know

Places near the Equator, the middle of the Earth, are usually hot. Places near the North Pole and South Pole are very cold.

🌴Hot and wet: rainforests (Brazil, Sri Lanka) — light clothes, houses open to the breeze
🏜️Hot and dry: deserts (Egypt) — loose clothes that cover the skin from the sun
❄️Cold: the Arctic (Nunavut) — warm coats, mitts, warm houses
🍂Four seasons: Ontario — summer is warm and winter is cold

People adapt: they change their clothes, homes and food to fit the place where they live.

Key words

  • Equator: an imaginary line around the middle of the Earth
  • North Pole: the farthest point north on Earth
  • adapt: to change to fit a place
  • season: a part of the year: spring, summer, fall or winter
