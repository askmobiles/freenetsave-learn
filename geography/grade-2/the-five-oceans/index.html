Learn what you need to know

🌊Pacific Ocean: the biggest and deepest; west of Canada
🌊Atlantic Ocean: east of Canada; between North America and Europe
🌊Indian Ocean: south of India and Sri Lanka
🧊Arctic Ocean: the smallest; north of Canada, very cold and icy
🐧Southern Ocean: all around Antarctica

Canada touches three oceans: the Pacific, the Atlantic and the Arctic. Canada has the longest coastline in the world!

Ocean water is salty. We cannot drink it. Whales, sharks, seals and many fish live in the oceans.

Key words

  • ocean: a very big body of salt water
  • coast: where the land meets the ocean
  • coastline: the edge of the land along the ocean
  • salt water: water with salt in it, like the ocean
