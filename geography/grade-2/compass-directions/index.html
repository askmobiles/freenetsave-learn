Compass directions

Learn what you need to know

⬆️North (N): usually at the top of a map
⬇️South (S): usually at the bottom
➡️East (E): usually on the right; the sun rises in the east
⬅️West (W): usually on the left; the sun sets in the west

Memory trick: Never Eat Soggy Waffles — North, East, South, West, going around clockwise.

Key words

  • compass: a tool with a needle that points north
  • compass rose: a drawing on a map that shows N, S, E and W
  • north: the direction toward the North Pole
  • direction: the way something is facing or going
