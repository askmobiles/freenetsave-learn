Global connections

Learn what you need to know

Trade is buying and selling goods and services between countries. Exports are sold to other countries; imports are bought from them. The balance of trade is exports minus imports.

🇺🇸The United States buys about three-quarters of Canada's exports
📦Top exports: crude oil, cars and parts, gold, lumber, potash, wheat
📥Top imports: cars and parts, machinery, electronics, clothing
🤝Trade agreements: CUSMA with the U.S. and Mexico; CETA with the European Union; CPTPP with Pacific countries

A tariff is a tax on imported goods. Tariffs can protect local jobs but make products cost more and can start trade disputes. In 2025 the United States placed tariffs on some Canadian goods such as steel, aluminum and cars.

Globalization means the world's countries are more connected through trade, travel, communication and culture. A supply chain is all the steps from raw material to finished product, often across several countries.

Canada also works with others through the United Nations, NATO, the G7, the Commonwealth and La Francophonie, and gives foreign aid.

Why it matters: about one in six Canadian jobs depends on exports.

Key words

  • export: a good or service sold to another country
  • import: a good or service bought from another country
  • tariff: a tax on imported goods
  • globalization: the growing connections between countries through trade, travel and communication
