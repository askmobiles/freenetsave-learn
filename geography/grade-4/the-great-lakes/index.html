The Great Lakes

Learn what you need to know

The Great Lakes are Superior, Michigan, Huron, Erie and Ontario. Remember them with HOMES.

🥇Lake Superior is the largest and deepest
🇺🇸Lake Michigan is the only one completely in the United States
🌊Niagara Falls is on the Niagara River, between Lake Erie and Lake Ontario
🚢Ships travel from the lakes to the Atlantic Ocean through the St. Lawrence Seaway
💧The lakes give drinking water to millions of people

Keep them clean: pollution and invasive species like zebra mussels are problems for the lakes.

Key words

  • fresh water: water that is not salty, like lakes and rivers
  • Great Lakes: five large connected lakes on the Canada–US border
  • invasive species: a plant or animal from somewhere else that harms local nature
  • seaway: a waterway that lets ships reach the sea
