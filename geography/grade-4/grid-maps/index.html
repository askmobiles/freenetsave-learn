Learn what you need to know

A grid map has lines that make squares. Letters go across the top (columns) and numbers go down the side (rows).

To find a square, go across to the letter, then down to the number. The school is in A1. The park is in C2.

Letter first, then number: "Walk along the hall, then go up the stairs."

Key words

  • grid: lines that cross to make squares
  • column: a line of squares going up and down, named by a letter
  • row: a line of squares going across, named by a number
  • coordinates: the letter and number that name a square
