Learn what you need to know

A capital city is where the government meets. Ottawa, Ontario, is the capital of Canada.

🌊British Columbia — Victoria
🛢️Alberta — Edmonton
🌾Saskatchewan — Regina
🐻‍❄️Manitoba — Winnipeg
🍁Ontario — Toronto
⚜️Quebec — Quebec City
🌉New Brunswick — Fredericton
⛵Nova Scotia — Halifax
🥔Prince Edward Island — Charlottetown
🐋Newfoundland and Labrador — St. John's
⛰️Yukon — Whitehorse
💎Northwest Territories — Yellowknife
🛷Nunavut — Iqaluit

Tricky ones: Victoria (not Vancouver) is BC's capital. Edmonton (not Calgary) is Alberta's capital. Toronto is Ontario's capital, but Ottawa is Canada's.

Key words

  • capital city: the city where a government meets
  • legislature: the building or group that makes laws for a province
  • Parliament: Canada's law-making body in Ottawa
  • federal: about the whole country
