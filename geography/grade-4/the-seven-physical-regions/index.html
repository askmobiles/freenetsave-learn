The seven physical regions

Canada has seven physical regions, each with its own landforms, climate and resources.⁠​​‌​‌​​​​‌‌​​​‌‌​​‌​‌​​‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌‌‌​​‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌‌​‌​​​‌‌‌​​‌‌​‌‌​​​​‌​‌‌‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌⁠ Grade 4 geography.

Learn what you need to know

A physical region is an area with similar landforms, climate and plants.

⛰️Cordillera: high mountains in the west (BC, Yukon) — forests, mining, tourism
🌾Interior Plains: flat land in the Prairies — farming, oil and gas
🪨Canadian Shield: old rock, lakes and forests — the biggest region; mining and hydro power
🦆Hudson Bay Lowlands: flat, wet land along Hudson Bay — wetlands and wildlife
🏙️Great Lakes–St. Lawrence Lowlands: rich soil, many cities — most Canadians live here
⛵Appalachian: old, rounded mountains in the east — fishing, forestry
🧊Arctic Lands: cold, treeless lands in the far north — permafrost and wildlife

Remember: the Canadian Shield is the largest region, and the Great Lakes–St. Lawrence Lowlands has the most people.

Key words

  • physical region: an area with similar landforms, climate and plants
  • landform: a natural shape of the land, like a mountain or plain
  • permafrost: ground that stays frozen all year
  • lowlands: low, mostly flat land
