British Columbia

British Columbia is Canada's west coast province, with ocean, rainforests and tall mountains. Learn its land, people and story.⁠​​‌​‌​​​​‌‌​​​‌‌​​‌​‌​​‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌‌‌​​‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌‌​‌​​​‌‌‌​​‌‌​‌‌​​​​‌​‌‌‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌⁠ Grade 4 geography.

Learn what you need to know

🏛️Capital: Victoria, on Vancouver Island
🏙️Biggest city: Vancouver
📅Joined Canada: 1871
🌸Flower: Pacific dogwood

The land: BC is mostly mountains. The Coast Mountains run along the ocean and the Rocky Mountains are on the Alberta side. The Fraser River is the longest river that is all inside BC. Off the coast are Vancouver Island and Haida Gwaii.

The people: First Nations have lived here for thousands of years. BC has more than 200 First Nations, such as the Haida, Musqueam and Secwépemc. Many people have come from Asia, Europe and all over the world.

The story: In 1858 gold was found on the Fraser River and thousands of miners rushed in. BC joined Canada in 1871 after Canada promised a railway to the Pacific. The Canadian Pacific Railway was finished in 1885. Chinese workers built some of the most dangerous parts.

Mild and wet: the coast has the mildest winters in Canada and lots of rain, so huge trees grow in the temperate rainforest.

Key words

  • temperate rainforest: a cool, very rainy forest with giant trees
  • gold rush: when many people hurry to a place where gold was found
  • coast: land beside the ocean
  • Confederation: when colonies joined together to make Canada
