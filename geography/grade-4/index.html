Canada

  1. 1Provinces and territoriesCanada has 10 provinces and 3 territories. Learn their names and where they are, from west to east.Ontario Grade 4 · Social Studies: People and Environments: Political and Physical Regions of Canada
  2. 2Capital cities of CanadaOttawa is Canada's capital, and every province and territory has its own capital city.Ontario Grade 4 · Social Studies: People and Environments: Political and Physical Regions of Canada
  3. 3The seven physical regionsCanada has seven physical regions, each with its own landforms, climate and resources.Ontario Grade 4 · Social Studies: People and Environments: Political and Physical Regions of Canada
  4. 4The Great LakesThe five Great Lakes hold about one-fifth of the world's fresh surface water. Learn their names and why they matter.Ontario Grade 4 · Social Studies: People and Environments: Political and Physical Regions of Canada
  5. 6Weather and climateWeather changes every day; climate is the usual weather of a place over many years. Learn why different parts of Canada have different climates.Ontario Grade 4 · Social Studies: People and Environments: Political and Physical Regions of Canada

My province

  1. 8British ColumbiaBritish Columbia is Canada's west coast province, with ocean, rainforests and tall mountains. Learn its land, people and story.Your province · BC classes study British Columbia in Social Studies 4 and 5
  2. 9AlbertaAlberta has the Rocky Mountains, wide prairies and some of the best dinosaur fossils in the world. Learn its land, people and story.Your province · Alberta classes study Alberta in Grade 4 Social Studies
  3. 10SaskatchewanSaskatchewan is famous for wheat fields, big skies and thousands of lakes. Learn its land, people and story.Your province · Saskatchewan classes study Saskatchewan in Grade 4 Social Studies
  4. 11ManitobaManitoba is in the middle of Canada, with prairie, big lakes and polar bears on Hudson Bay. Learn its land, people and story.Your province · Manitoba classes study Manitoba in Grade 4 Social Studies
  5. 12QuebecQuebec is Canada's largest province and home of most French-speaking Canadians. Learn its land, people and story.Your province · Quebec classes study Quebec in elementary Geography, History and Citizenship
  6. 13New BrunswickNew Brunswick is Canada's only officially bilingual province, with forests, rivers and the highest tides in the world. Learn its land, people and story.Your province · New Brunswick classes study their province in elementary Social Studies (usually Grade 3 or 4)
  7. 14Nova ScotiaNova Scotia is almost surrounded by the sea, with fishing villages, lighthouses and a long history. Learn its land, people and story.Your province · Nova Scotia classes study Nova Scotia in Social Studies 3
  8. 15Prince Edward IslandPrince Edward Island is Canada's smallest province, with red soil, beaches and potato farms. Learn its land, people and story.Your province · PEI classes study Prince Edward Island in Social Studies 3
  9. 16Newfoundland and LabradorNewfoundland and Labrador is Canada's most easterly province and the last to join. Learn its land, people and story.Your province · Newfoundland and Labrador classes study their province in Social Studies 3 and 4
  10. 17YukonYukon is Canada's westernmost territory, home of Canada's highest mountain and the Klondike Gold Rush. Learn its land, people and story.Your territory · Yukon classes use the BC curriculum and study Yukon in elementary Social Studies
  11. 18Northwest TerritoriesThe Northwest Territories has giant lakes, Canada's longest river and 11 official languages. Learn its land, people and story.Your territory · NWT classes use the Alberta curriculum and study the NWT in Grade 4 Social Studies
  12. 19NunavutNunavut is Canada's newest and largest territory, the homeland of the Inuit. Learn its land, people and story.Your territory · Nunavut classes study Nunavut and Inuit life in elementary Social Studies
