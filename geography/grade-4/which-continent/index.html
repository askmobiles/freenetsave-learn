Learn what you need to know

🌎North America: Canada, United States, Mexico
🌎South America: Brazil, Argentina, Peru
🌍Europe: France, Italy, United Kingdom
🌍Africa: Egypt, Kenya, Nigeria
🌏Asia: India, China, Sri Lanka, Japan
🌏Oceania (Australia): Australia, New Zealand, Fiji
🐧Antarctica: no countries; scientists visit

Asia is the largest continent and has the most people. Australia (Oceania) is the smallest. Antarctica is the coldest.

Canada is on the continent of North America.

Key words

  • continent: one of the seven very large areas of land on Earth
  • country: a nation with its own government and borders
  • equator: an imaginary line around the middle of the Earth
  • ocean: a very large body of salt water
