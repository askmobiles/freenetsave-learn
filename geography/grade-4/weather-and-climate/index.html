Weather and climate

Weather changes every day; climate is the usual weather of a place over many years. Learn why different parts of Canada have different climates.⁠​​‌​‌​​​​‌‌​​​‌‌​​‌​‌​​‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌‌‌​​‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌‌​‌​​​‌‌‌​​‌‌​‌‌​​​​‌​‌‌‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌⁠ Grade 4 geography.

Learn what you need to know

🌦️Weather: what the air is like today — rain, sun, wind, temperature
📅Climate: the usual weather of a place over 30 years or more

What affects climate?

🧭Latitude: places farther north are colder
🌊Near water: oceans and big lakes keep places milder in winter and cooler in summer
⛰️Height: it is colder high in the mountains
💨Winds: winds from the ocean bring rain; winds from the Arctic bring cold

Examples: Victoria, BC, has mild, rainy winters because of the Pacific Ocean. Winnipeg, far from oceans, has very cold winters and hot summers. Iqaluit, in the far north, is cold most of the year.

Quick test: "It is snowing today" = weather. "Winters in Winnipeg are very cold" = climate.

Key words

  • weather: the condition of the air at a certain time and place
  • climate: the usual weather of a place over many years
  • latitude: how far north or south a place is from the equator
  • precipitation: water that falls from the sky: rain, snow, sleet or hail
