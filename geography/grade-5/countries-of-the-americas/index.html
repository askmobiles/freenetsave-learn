Countries of the Americas

Learn what you need to know

🍁North America: Canada (Ottawa), United States (Washington, D.C.), Mexico (Mexico City)
🌋Central America: Guatemala, Costa Rica, Panama (the Panama Canal)
🏝️Caribbean: Jamaica (Kingston), Cuba (Havana), Trinidad and Tobago
🌳South America: Brazil (Brasília), Argentina (Buenos Aires), Peru (Lima), Chile (Santiago), Colombia (Bogotá)

Fun facts: Brazil is the largest country in South America and has most of the Amazon rainforest. Chile is long and thin. The Andes are the longest mountain range on land.

Languages: English, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil) and French are widely spoken in the Americas.

Key words

  • the Americas: North, Central and South America and the Caribbean together
  • Caribbean: a sea and its islands south-east of North America
  • Amazon: the largest rainforest in the world, in South America
  • canal: a human-made waterway for ships
