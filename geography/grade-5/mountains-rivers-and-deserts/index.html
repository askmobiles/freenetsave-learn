Learn what you need to know

🏔️Mount Everest (Asia): highest mountain above sea level, about 8,849 m
⛰️Mount Logan (Yukon): Canada's highest mountain, about 5,959 m
🌊The Nile (Africa) and the Amazon (South America): the two longest rivers
🏜️Sahara (Africa): the largest hot desert
🧊Antarctica: the largest desert of all — a cold desert with very little precipitation

A desert is a place that gets very little rain or snow (less than about 250 mm a year). Deserts can be hot or cold!

People adapt: farmers grow food along the Nile, mountain people raise llamas in the Andes, and desert peoples find water at oases.

Key words

  • desert: a place with very little precipitation
  • oasis: a place in a desert with water and plants
  • summit: the highest point of a mountain
  • sea level: the height of the ocean surface, used to measure land height
