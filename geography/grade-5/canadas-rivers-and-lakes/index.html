Learn what you need to know

🏞️Mackenzie River: Canada's longest river; flows north to the Arctic Ocean
🚢St. Lawrence River: connects the Great Lakes to the Atlantic; a busy shipping route
🐟Fraser River: BC's great salmon river; flows to the Pacific
🌊Great Bear Lake (NWT): the largest lake entirely in Canada
🧭Lake Winnipeg: a huge lake in Manitoba
🦫Canada has more lakes than any other country — over two million!

Why rivers matter: Indigenous peoples and fur traders travelled by canoe on rivers. Today rivers give us drinking water, hydroelectric power, fish and transportation.

Drainage basin: all the land whose water drains into one river or ocean. Much of Canada drains into Hudson Bay.

Key words

  • drainage basin: all the land that drains into one river, lake or ocean
  • hydroelectricity: electricity made from moving water
  • mouth: where a river flows into a lake or ocean
  • source: where a river begins
