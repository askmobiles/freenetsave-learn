The Earth in space

Learn what you need to know

🌍Rotation: Earth spins once every 24 hours, causing day and night
☀️Revolution: Earth travels around the Sun once every 365¼ days — one year
📐Tilt: Earth is tilted about 23.5°, which causes the seasons
🕐Time zones: because Earth rotates, it is different times in different places; Canada has six time zones

In June, the Northern Hemisphere tilts toward the Sun, so Canada has summer. In December, it tilts away, so Canada has winter — and Australia has summer!

Leap year: the extra ¼ day each year adds up, so every 4 years February gets a 29th day.

Key words

  • rotation: spinning around on an axis
  • revolution: travelling around something in an orbit
  • axis: an imaginary line through the North and South Poles that Earth spins around
  • time zone: a region that uses the same clock time
