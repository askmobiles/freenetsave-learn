Latitude and longitude

Use imaginary lines of latitude and longitude to find any place on Earth.⁠​​‌​‌​​​​‌‌​​​‌‌​​‌​‌​​‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌‌‌​​‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌‌​‌​​​‌‌‌​​‌‌​‌‌​​​​‌​‌‌‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌⁠ Grade 5 geography.

Learn what you need to know

➖Latitude lines run across (east–west) and measure how far north or south a place is
0️⃣The Equator is 0° latitude; the North Pole is 90°N and the South Pole is 90°S
➕Longitude lines run from pole to pole and measure how far east or west a place is
🇬🇧The Prime Meridian is 0° longitude; it passes through Greenwich, England
📍Write latitude first, then longitude: Toronto is about 44°N, 79°W

Memory trick: LATitude lines lie FLAT like the rungs of a ladder (ladder → "lat-der"). LONGitude lines are LONG and go from pole to pole.

Key words

  • latitude: distance north or south of the Equator, in degrees
  • longitude: distance east or west of the Prime Meridian, in degrees
  • Equator: the 0° line of latitude around the middle of the Earth
  • Prime Meridian: the 0° line of longitude through Greenwich, England
