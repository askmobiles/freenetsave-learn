Learn what you need to know

🇨🇳China: Beijing
🇮🇳India: New Delhi
🇯🇵Japan: Tokyo
🇰🇷South Korea: Seoul
🇵🇭Philippines: Manila
🇱🇰Sri Lanka: Sri Jayawardenepura Kotte (Colombo is the largest city)
🇵🇰Pakistan: Islamabad
🇻🇳Vietnam: Hanoi
🇪🇬Egypt: Cairo
🇳🇬Nigeria: Abuja
🇰🇪Kenya: Nairobi
🇪🇹Ethiopia: Addis Ababa
🇬🇭Ghana: Accra
🇿🇦South Africa: three capitals, including Pretoria
🇲🇦Morocco: Rabat

Watch out: the biggest city is not always the capital. Lagos is bigger than Abuja, Karachi is bigger than Islamabad, and Casablanca is bigger than Rabat.

Key words

  • capital: the city where a country's government meets
  • population: the number of people living in a place
  • Sahara: the large desert across northern Africa
  • Himalayas: the highest mountains, in Asia
