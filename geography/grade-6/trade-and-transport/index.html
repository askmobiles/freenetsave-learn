Learn what you need to know

Trade means buying and selling goods and services. Exports are what Canada sells to other countries. Imports are what Canada buys from them.

📤Canada exports: oil and gas, cars, wheat and canola, lumber, minerals, seafood, maple syrup
📥Canada imports: electronics, clothing, fruit and vegetables in winter, machinery
🇺🇸The United States is Canada's biggest trading partner by far
🤝Trade agreements: CUSMA (Canada, US, Mexico) and agreements with Europe and Asia-Pacific countries
🚢Ships: ports of Vancouver, Montreal, Halifax and Prince Rupert
🚂Trains: carry grain, coal and containers across the country
🚚Trucks: cross the border at places like the Ambassador Bridge
✈️Planes: fast for fresh food and small valuable goods

Interdependence: countries depend on each other. Your phone might be designed in one country, built in another and sold in Canada.

Key words

  • export: a good sold to another country
  • import: a good bought from another country
  • trading partner: a country you buy from and sell to
  • interdependence: when countries depend on each other
