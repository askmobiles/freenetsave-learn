Caring for the Earth

Learn what you need to know

🌡️Climate change: Earth is warming, mainly from burning fossil fuels
🧴Plastic pollution: plastic waste ends up in oceans and harms wildlife
🌳Deforestation: cutting down forests faster than they grow back
🐼Endangered species: animals and plants at risk of disappearing

Working together: environmental problems cross borders, so countries make agreements. The Paris Agreement (2015) is a global plan to slow climate change. The Montreal Protocol (1987), signed in Canada, helped protect the ozone layer.

What you can do: reduce, reuse, recycle; walk, bike or take transit; save energy and water; plant trees; and speak up in your school and community.

Key words

  • fossil fuels: coal, oil and gas, which release carbon dioxide when burned
  • deforestation: clearing forests faster than they regrow
  • endangered: at risk of disappearing forever
  • ozone layer: a layer of gas high in the air that blocks harmful UV rays
