Learn what you need to know

🌍Seven continents: Asia, Africa, North America, South America, Antarctica, Europe, Australia (Oceania)
🌊Five oceans: Pacific, Atlantic, Indian, Southern, Arctic
🧭Four hemispheres: Northern, Southern (split by the Equator); Eastern, Western (split by the Prime Meridian)

Biggest and smallest: Asia is the largest continent and has the most people. Australia is the smallest. The Pacific is the largest and deepest ocean; the Arctic is the smallest.

Relative location describes a place compared with other places ("Canada is north of the United States"). Absolute location uses exact coordinates (45°N, 75°W).

Key words

  • continent: one of the seven large land masses on Earth
  • hemisphere: half of the Earth
  • relative location: where a place is compared with other places
  • absolute location: the exact position using latitude and longitude
