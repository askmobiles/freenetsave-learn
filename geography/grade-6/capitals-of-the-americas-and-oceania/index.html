Oceania is the region of Australia, New Zealand and thousands of Pacific islands. The Indigenous Māori people of New Zealand and Aboriginal peoples of Australia have lived there for thousands of years.

Key words

  • Oceania: the region of Australia, New Zealand and the Pacific islands
  • island nation: a country made up of one or more islands
  • Māori: the Indigenous people of New Zealand
  • Commonwealth: a group of countries with historic ties to Britain, including Canada and Australia
