Learn what you need to know

🌴Tropical: hot and wet all year, near the Equator (Amazon, Congo)
🏜️Dry (desert): very little rain (Sahara, central Australia)
🌳Temperate: warm summers and cool or cold winters (southern Canada, Europe)
🌲Continental/Subarctic: long cold winters, short summers (northern Canada, Russia)
🧊Polar: very cold all year (Arctic, Antarctica)
🏔️Highland (mountain): colder as you go higher (Rockies, Himalayas)

What shapes climate? Latitude (distance from the Equator), height above sea level, nearness to water, ocean currents and wind patterns.

Weather vs climate: weather is what happens today; climate is the usual pattern over many years.

Key words

  • climate: the usual weather of a place over many years
  • weather: the conditions in the air today
  • tropical: hot and wet, near the Equator
  • polar: very cold all year, near the poles
