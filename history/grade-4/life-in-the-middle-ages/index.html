Learn what you need to know

The Middle Ages (or medieval times) in Europe lasted from about 500 to 1500 CE, after the fall of the Western Roman Empire.

👑King: ruled the land and gave land to lords
🏰Lords and nobles: lived in castles and manors
⚔️Knights: trained soldiers on horseback who served lords
🌾Peasants and serfs: farmed the land and gave crops to the lord

This system was called feudalism: land was traded for loyalty, protection and work. Most people were peasants and lived in small villages.

Castles had thick stone walls, towers, a moat and a drawbridge for protection. Monks in monasteries copied books by hand. Later, towns grew with markets and guilds of craftspeople.

Remember: feudalism = king → lords → knights → peasants. Castles protected people in war.

Key words

  • feudalism: a system where land was given in return for service and loyalty
  • serf: a peasant who could not leave the lord's land
  • moat: a deep ditch of water around a castle
  • guild: a group of craftspeople in a town
