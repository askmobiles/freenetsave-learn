Learn what you need to know

A civilization is a large group of people with cities, leaders, laws, jobs, religion and often writing. The first ones grew near rivers, where farmers could grow lots of food.

🏺Mesopotamia (Tigris and Euphrates rivers): one of the first systems of writing, cuneiform
🔺Ancient Egypt (Nile River): pyramids, pharaohs and hieroglyphs
🏙️Indus Valley (Indus River): planned cities like Mohenjo-daro with drains
🐉Ancient China (Yellow River): silk, writing on bones and later the Great Wall
🏛️Ancient Greece: Olympic Games and early democracy in Athens
🛣️Ancient Rome: roads, aqueducts and the Colosseum
🌽The Maya (Central America): calendars, pyramids and writing
🛞Inventions: the wheel, plough, calendars and writing

Farming let people stay in one place. Extra food meant some people could do other jobs: builders, priests, traders and rulers.

Remember: early civilizations grew near rivers and gave us writing, laws, calendars and inventions we still use.

Key words

  • civilization: a large society with cities, leaders and laws
  • hieroglyphs: picture writing of ancient Egypt
  • pharaoh: a ruler of ancient Egypt
  • democracy: government where citizens have a say
