Learn what you need to know

A right is something you are allowed to have or do. A responsibility is something you should do to be a good citizen.

Canada's rights are protected by the Canadian Charter of Rights and Freedoms, part of the Constitution since 1982.

🗣️Fundamental freedoms: religion, expression, peaceful assembly
🗳️Democratic rights: citizens 18+ can vote
✈️Mobility rights: live and work anywhere in Canada
⚖️Legal rights: a fair trial, a lawyer
🤝Equality rights: no discrimination for race, religion, sex, age, disability…
🇫🇷Language rights: English and French services federally

Responsibilities include obeying laws, voting, respecting other people's rights, helping your community and protecting the environment.

Rights have limits: free speech does not allow you to spread hate or threaten others.

Key words

  • right: a freedom or protection everyone is entitled to
  • responsibility: a duty you have as a member of a community
  • Charter of Rights and Freedoms: the part of the Constitution that protects rights in Canada (1982)
  • discrimination: unfair treatment because of who someone is

School rules
