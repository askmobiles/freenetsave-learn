Learn what you need to know

France started Port-Royal in Acadia (Nova Scotia) in 1605 and Quebec in 1608. At first New France grew slowly: it was mostly a fur-trading post.

In 1663, King Louis XIV made New France a royal colony, run by a governor (army and relations with other nations), an intendant (money, justice, settlement — Jean Talon was the first) and a bishop (the Church).

🏰Seigneur: received land from the king and divided it among settlers
🌾Habitants: farmers who rented long, narrow strips of land by the river
⛪Church: schools, hospitals and religion
👰Filles du roi: about 800 women sent 1663–1673 to marry settlers and grow the colony

The seigneurial system gave each farm a strip of land facing the river, because the river was the main road.

Population: New France had only about 3000 settlers in 1663, but about 70 000 by 1760.

Key words

  • seigneur: a landowner in New France who divided land among settlers
  • habitant: a farmer in New France
  • intendant: the official who ran the colony's money, justice and settlement
  • filles du roi: "the king's daughters": women sent to New France to marry and start families
