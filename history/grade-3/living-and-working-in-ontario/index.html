Learn what you need to know

Ontario has big cities, small towns, farms, forests and thousands of lakes. How people use the land is called land use.

🌽Farming in the south: corn, soybeans, fruit and dairy
🌲Forestry in the north: lumber and paper
⛏️Mining: nickel at Sudbury, gold at Timmins
🏭Manufacturing: cars made in Oshawa, Windsor and other places

The Great Lakes give Ontario drinking water, ships to carry goods, fishing and fun. Niagara Falls makes hydroelectric power and brings many tourists.

Using land and water changes the environment. Ontario has rules and provincial parks such as Algonquin to protect nature, and towns plan where homes, farms and factories go.

Remember: Ontario's land and water give us jobs, but we must protect them too.

Key words

  • land use: how people use an area of land
  • industry: a group of businesses that make or do the same kind of thing
  • natural resource: something from nature people use, like trees or water
  • municipality: a city, town or township with its own local government
