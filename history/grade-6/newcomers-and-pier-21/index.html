Learn what you need to know

Immigrants choose to move to a new country. Refugees are forced to flee danger, war or persecution.

➡️Push factors: war, poverty, lack of jobs, unfair treatment at home
⬅️Pull factors: jobs, safety, freedom, education, family already in Canada

From 1928 to 1971, about one million immigrants, refugees and war brides arrived through Pier 21 in Halifax. Today it is the Canadian Museum of Immigration.

⛵1979–1980: about 60 000 Vietnamese, Cambodian and Laotian refugees, many sponsored by ordinary Canadians
🏠2015–2016: about 25 000 Syrian refugees in a few months, and more after
✈️Today: most newcomers arrive by plane at airports like Toronto Pearson

Newcomers face challenges — a new language, finding work, cold winters — but they build businesses, communities and new traditions that become part of Canada.

Private sponsorship: Canada lets groups of citizens sponsor refugees, which is rare in the world.

Key words

  • immigrant: a person who moves to live permanently in another country
  • refugee: a person forced to flee their country because of danger
  • push factor: a reason that pushes people to leave a place
  • pull factor: a reason that attracts people to a new place
