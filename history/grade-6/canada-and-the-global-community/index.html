Learn what you need to know

The global community means all the countries and people of the world. Canada works with others to solve problems no country can fix alone.

🇺🇳United Nations (UN): Canada was a founding member in 1945
🕊️Peacekeeping: Lester B. Pearson won the Nobel Peace Prize (1957) for creating UN peacekeeping
🤝Foreign aid: money and help for countries facing poverty, disaster or war
🚢Trade: Canada sells oil, cars, wheat and more; the US is its biggest partner (CUSMA)
🌍Global issues: climate change, human rights, refugees, health

Canada also belongs to NATO (a defence alliance), the G7, the Commonwealth and La Francophonie.

Non-governmental organizations (NGOs) like the Red Cross, UNICEF and Doctors Without Borders also help around the world. Canadian students raise money and awareness too.

Global citizen: someone who cares about people and problems beyond their own country.

Key words

  • United Nations: an organization of almost every country working for peace and cooperation
  • peacekeeping: sending neutral troops to help keep peace after conflicts
  • foreign aid: help one country gives another
  • NGO: non-governmental organization: a group that helps people but is not a government

Disaster relief
