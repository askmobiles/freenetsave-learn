Learn what you need to know

History is shaped by many people: leaders and ordinary people, men and women, Indigenous and newcomer.

🪶Joseph Brant (Thayendanegea): Mohawk leader, brought Six Nations Loyalists to the Grand River
🎖️Isaac Brock: defended Upper Canada in 1812
🪶Tecumseh: Shawnee leader who allied with Britain in 1812
🚶‍♀️Laura Secord: warned of the attack at Beaver Dams
🔥William Lyon Mackenzie and Louis-Joseph Papineau: led the 1837 rebellions
⚖️Robert Baldwin and Louis-Hippolyte LaFontaine: won responsible government
✊🏿Marie-Joseph Angélique: enslaved woman whose story shows slavery in New France
🪶Shanawdithit: one of the last known Beothuk, died 1829; her drawings preserve their history

To judge a person's historical significance, ask: Did they cause change? How many people did it affect? Do their actions still matter today?

Different perspectives: a hero to one group may be seen differently by another. Brock is a hero in Canada; in the US he was an enemy general.

Key words

  • historical significance: how important a person or event is, based on its impact
  • Beothuk: Indigenous people of Newfoundland who did not survive colonization
  • reformer: a person who works to improve a system without overthrowing it
  • primary source: evidence created at the time, like letters, drawings or artifacts
