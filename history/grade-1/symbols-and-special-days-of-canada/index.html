Learn what you need to know

A symbol is a picture or object that stands for something. Canada has many symbols that help us show we are Canadian.

🍁Maple leaf: on our red and white flag
🦫Beaver: a national animal of Canada
🪙Loonie: the $1 coin with a loon bird
🎵"O Canada": our national anthem

Canada Day is on July 1. It is Canada's birthday, from when Canada became a country in 1867. People have parades, picnics and fireworks.

Other special days: Remembrance Day (November 11) when we remember people who served in wars, and National Indigenous Peoples Day (June 21).

Remember: the maple leaf, the beaver and the loonie are symbols of Canada. Canada Day is July 1.

Key words

  • symbol: something that stands for something else
  • flag: cloth with a design that stands for a country
  • anthem: a country's special song
  • celebrate: to do something special for a happy day
