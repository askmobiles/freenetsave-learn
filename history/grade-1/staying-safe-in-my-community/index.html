Learn what you need to know

Staying safe means knowing safety rules and knowing who to ask for help.

☎️In an emergency, call 911
🚦Stop, look both ways and listen before you cross
🔥Stop, drop and roll if your clothes catch fire
🚒Know two ways out of your home, and a meeting place outside

An emergency is when someone is badly hurt, there is a fire, or there is danger right now. Call 911 only for real emergencies, and know your home address.

Safe helpers include police officers, firefighters, paramedics, teachers and crossing guards. Never go anywhere with a stranger.

Remember: 911 for emergencies. Stop, look, listen before crossing. Stop, drop and roll.

Key words

  • emergency: danger or a bad injury that needs help right away
  • 911: the number to call in an emergency
  • paramedic: a helper who gives first aid and drives the ambulance
  • stranger: a person you do not know
