Learn what you need to know

A rule tells us how to act so everyone stays safe and is treated fairly. A responsibility is a job that is ours to do.

🏠At home: tidy your toys, help set the table, feed the pet
🏫At school: listen, take turns, put things back
🌳In the park: throw garbage in the bin, be kind to others
🚸On the street: hold a grown-up's hand, cross at the crosswalk

Our roles change as we grow. A baby cannot do many jobs. A Grade 1 student can do more, like getting dressed and packing a backpack.

When we follow rules and do our jobs, people trust us and our home, class and community work better.

Remember: rules keep us safe and fair. Responsibilities are the jobs we do to help.

Key words

  • rule: tells us what to do or not do
  • responsibility: a job that is ours to do
  • role: the part we play, like student or helper
  • fair: treating everyone the right way
