Learn what you need to know

A community is a place where people live, work and play together. It has homes, schools, stores, parks and many other places.

🏥Hospital: doctors and nurses help sick people
🚒Fire station: firefighters put out fires
📚Library: librarians help us find books
🏪Store: workers sell food and things we need

Community helpers do jobs that help everyone: police officers, crossing guards, bus drivers, mail carriers, teachers, cleaners and farmers.

Some places are natural, like parks, rivers and forests. Others are built by people, like roads, schools and bridges. We take care of both.

Remember: a community is people and places. Helpers make it work.

Key words

  • community: a place where people live, work and play
  • community helper: a person whose job helps others
  • natural: made by nature
  • built: made by people
