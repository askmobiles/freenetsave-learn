Learn what you need to know

Long ago means many years before we were born. Now means today. Some things have changed a lot, and some things stay the same.

📞Then: phones with cords. Now: mobile phones
🐴Then: horses and wagons. Now: cars, buses and planes
🕯️Then: candles and oil lamps. Now: electric lights
📮Then: letters by mail. Now: video calls and messages

Some things stay the same: families love each other, children play, and people eat together and celebrate special days.

We learn about long ago from old photos, grandparents' stories, museums and old objects called artifacts.

Remember: things change over time. We can learn about the past by asking older people and looking at old things.

Key words

  • past: the time that has already happened
  • present: the time now
  • change: when something becomes different
  • artifact: an old object that tells us about the past
