Learn what you need to know

A tradition is something a family or group does again and again, often passed down from grandparents to children. A celebration is a happy time to mark a special day.

🎂Birthdays: cake, candles and wishes
🍽️Family meals: special foods on special days
💃Dances and songs from our culture
🎁Giving gifts or cards to show we care

Traditions can change over time. A family might add new foods, or celebrate a holiday from a friend's culture too.

In Canada, people come from all over the world, so there are many traditions. Learning about them helps us respect each other.

Remember: traditions are passed down. Celebrations help families and communities feel close.

Key words

  • tradition: something done again and again and passed down
  • celebration: a happy time to mark a special day
  • culture: the way of life of a group of people
  • heritage: traditions and things passed down from the past
