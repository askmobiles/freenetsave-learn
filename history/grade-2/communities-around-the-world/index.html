Learn what you need to know

People live in communities all over the world. The climate (weather over a long time) and the land change how they live.

🧊Cold places (Nunavut, Canada): warm clothes, snowmobiles, homes built to keep heat in
🌴Hot places (Kenya): light clothes, homes that stay cool
🏜️Dry places (Egypt): little rain, farming near the Nile River
🌧️Wet places (Bangladesh): heavy rains, some homes on stilts

People in every community need the same basic needs: food, water, shelter and clothing. They meet these needs in different ways.

Children around the world go to school, play games, help their families and celebrate with friends, just like you.

Remember: climate and land shape how people live, but all people have the same basic needs.

Key words

  • climate: the usual weather of a place over a long time
  • basic needs: food, water, shelter and clothing
  • shelter: a place to live that protects us
  • global: about the whole world
