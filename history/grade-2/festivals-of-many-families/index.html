Learn what you need to know

Families in Canada celebrate many festivals. Each festival has its own stories, foods and ways to celebrate.

🏮Lunar New Year: red envelopes, dragon dances and family dinners
🪔Diwali: the festival of lights, with lamps (diyas) and sweets
🌙Eid al-Fitr: a celebration after Ramadan, with prayers, new clothes and feasts
🕎Hanukkah: lighting the menorah for eight nights
🎄Christmas: trees, carols and gifts
🍚Thai Pongal: a Tamil harvest festival in January, with sweet pongal rice
🕯️Kwanzaa: seven candles for seven principles
🎆Vaisakhi: a Sikh and Punjabi harvest festival in April

Many festivals share ideas: light, family, giving thanks, sharing food and helping others.

Remember: different festivals, many of the same ideas: family, light, food and thanks.

Key words

  • festival: a special time of celebration
  • harvest: gathering crops when they are ready
  • menorah: a candle holder used at Hanukkah
  • diya: a small oil lamp used at Diwali
