Learn what you need to know

A holiday is a special day when many people do not go to work or school. Each holiday has a reason and a story.

🎆Canada Day (July 1): Canada became a country in 1867
🦃Thanksgiving (2nd Monday of October in Canada): giving thanks for the harvest
🌺Remembrance Day (November 11): remembering those who served in wars
🪶National Indigenous Peoples Day (June 21): honouring First Nations, Inuit and Métis

Family Day (third Monday of February in Ontario) is a day to spend time with family. Victoria Day (in May) celebrates Queen Victoria's birthday. Labour Day (September) honours workers.

September 30 is the National Day for Truth and Reconciliation. People wear orange shirts to remember children who were sent to residential schools.

Remember: every holiday has a reason. Canada Day is July 1 and Remembrance Day is November 11.

Key words

  • holiday: a special day, often with no work or school
  • harvest: gathering crops that are ready
  • remember: to keep in our minds and hearts
  • reconciliation: making things right after harm
