Learn what you need to know

Your family history is the story of your family: who they are, where they lived and what they did. Your ancestors are family members who lived long before you.

🌳Family tree: a chart of parents, grandparents and great-grandparents
🗺️Many Canadian families came from other countries
🗣️Languages: some families speak more than one
📷Old photos and stories help us learn our history

Canada is home to First Nations, Inuit and Métis peoples, who have lived here for thousands of years, and to families who came from every part of the world.

Your heritage includes your family's language, foods, music, clothes and traditions.

Remember: a family tree shows who came before you. Every family has a story.

Key words

  • ancestor: a family member who lived long ago
  • family tree: a chart showing how family members are related
  • immigrant: a person who moves to a new country to live
  • generation: people born around the same time in a family
