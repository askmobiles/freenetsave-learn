Learn what you need to know

Besides the maple leaf flag, Canada has many other symbols. Each province and territory has its own flag, flower and bird too.

🌸Ontario's flower: the white trillium
🏛️Parliament in Ottawa: where Canada's laws are made
🐎RCMP officers in red serge on horseback
🦌Caribou: on the 25-cent coin (quarter)

The Inuksuk, a stone figure built by Inuit, is a symbol of the North. It can show the way or mark a special place.

Canada has two official languages: English and French. You see both on cereal boxes, coins and signs.

Remember: each province has its own symbols. Ontario's flower is the trillium.

Key words

  • province: one of Canada's ten large areas, like Ontario
  • territory: one of Canada's three northern areas
  • Parliament: where Canada's laws are made
  • inuksuk: an Inuit stone figure that shows the way
