History lessons

Full lessons that teach each topic step by step, with pictures, real-life examples, practice, a game, a test and a challenge. Lessons for Grades 1 to 8 can be read aloud by a helper, and every lesson can be printed as a worksheet with an answer key.

Each grade follows the Ontario curriculum (📘) and adds ⭐ extra topics taught in other provinces and countries, so students, parents and teachers can choose what to learn or teach.

  1. 1Grade 1 History5 lessons: Rules and responsibilities, People and places in my community, Then and now, Staying safe in my community and more
  2. 2Grade 2 History6 lessons: Celebrations and traditions, Communities around the world, Family history and heritage, Festivals of many families and more
  3. 3Grade 3 History5 lessons: Who lived here around 1800, Building a home in the forest, Work, school and travel, Living and working in Ontario and more
  4. 4Grade 4 History5 lessons: Ancient civilizations, First Nations, Inuit and Métis, Life in the Middle Ages, Remembrance Day: how we remember and more
  5. 5Grade 5 History6 lessons: The First Peoples, Explorers arrive, New France begins, The Wendat, missions and the fur trade and more
  6. 6Grade 6 History5 lessons: Communities that make up Canada, Unfair laws and apologies, Rights grow, Canada and the global community and more
  7. 7Grade 7 History8 lessons: Life in New France, Britain takes New France, Loyalists and new beginnings, The War of 1812 and more
  8. 8Grade 8 History7 lessons: The road to Confederation, Canada grows from sea to sea, Treaties, the Indian Act and residential schools, The railway and 1885 and more
  9. 10Grade 10 History9 lessons: Canada in the First World War, Rights and change after the war, The Great Depression, Canada in the Second World War and more
