Core French: Le, la, les as pronouns

Avoid repeating a noun: Tu aimes ce film? Oui, je <b>l'</b>aime.⁠​​‌​‌​​​​‌‌​​​‌‌​​‌​‌​​‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌‌‌​​‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌‌​‌​​​‌‌‌​​‌‌​‌‌​​​​‌​‌‌‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌⁠ Grade 9 French.

Learn what you need to know

🔁le = it/him (masc.), la = it/her (fem.), l' = before a vowel, les = them
📍Place: BEFORE the verb. Je regarde la télé → Je la regarde.
➖Negative: Je ne la regarde pas.
➡️With two verbs: before the infinitive. Je vais le faire.

Big difference from English: English puts "it" after the verb (I like it). French puts it before (Je l'aime).

Key words

  • le pronom: pronoun
  • le / la: it, him / her
  • les: them
  • l': it (before a vowel)
