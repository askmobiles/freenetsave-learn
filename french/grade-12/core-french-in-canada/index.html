Core French: French in Canada and bilingualism

Learn what you need to know

📜1969: the Official Languages Act makes English and French official languages of the federal government
⚖️1982: the Charter of Rights protects minority-language education rights (section 23)
🏫Ontario has French-language school boards and French Immersion and Core French programs
🗺️Francophone communities: Québec, Acadie (N.B., N.S., P.E.I.), Franco-Ontarians, Franco-Manitobans, Fransaskois, Franco-Albertans
🗣️Canadian French words: un char (car, informal), magasiner (to shop), une fin de semaine (weekend), un dépanneur (corner store)

Bilingual = two languages. Being bilingual in English and French opens doors to jobs in government, tourism, health care and international work.

Key words

  • bilingue: bilingual
  • une langue officielle: official language
  • magasiner: to shop (Canadian)
  • un dépanneur: corner store (Québec)
