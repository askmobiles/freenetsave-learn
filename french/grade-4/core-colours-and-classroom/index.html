Learn what you need to know

✏️un crayon = a pencil
🖊️un stylo = a pen
📏une règle = a ruler
📓un cahier = a notebook
📕un livre = a book
🎒un sac à dos = a backpack
🪑une chaise = a chair
🧽une gomme = an eraser
🔴rouge
🔵bleu
🟡jaune
🟢vert
⚫noir
⚪blanc
🟠orange
🩷rose

Two big rules: 1) Every noun is masculine (un) or feminine (une). 2) The colour goes after the noun: un crayon rouge (a red pencil).

Key words

  • un / une: a (masculine / feminine)
  • un crayon: a pencil
  • une règle: a ruler
  • rouge: red
