Learn what you need to know

👋Bonjour! = Hello!
🙂Salut! = Hi! (with friends)
😀Je m'appelle Ava. = My name is Ava.
❓Comment t'appelles-tu? = What is your name?
🎂J'ai neuf ans. = I am nine years old.
❓Quel âge as-tu? = How old are you?
👍Ça va? — Ça va bien, merci! = How are you? — I'm fine, thanks!
👋Au revoir! = Goodbye!

Remember: in French you have your age: J'ai neuf ans (I have nine years). Never say « Je suis neuf ».

Key words

  • bonjour: hello
  • je m'appelle: my name is
  • j'ai … ans: I am … years old
  • au revoir: goodbye
