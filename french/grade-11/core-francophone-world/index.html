Learn what you need to know

🇨🇦In Canada: Québec, New Brunswick (Acadie), Ontario (Franco-Ontarians), Manitoba and communities across the country
🌍Africa has the most French speakers: Côte d'Ivoire, Sénégal, RD Congo, Cameroun, Maroc
🇪🇺Europe: France, Belgique, Suisse, Luxembourg, Monaco
🌴Also: Haïti, Martinique, Guadeloupe, Polynésie française, Vietnam (historically)
🤝La Francophonie: an organization of countries that share French
🗓️March 20 is the International Day of the Francophonie

Over 300 million people speak French, and many of them live in Africa. Learning French connects you to the whole world.

Key words

  • la Francophonie: French-speaking world
  • un francophone: a French speaker
  • l'Acadie: Acadia (Maritimes)
  • franco-ontarien: Franco-Ontarian
