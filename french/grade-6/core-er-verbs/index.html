Core French: -ER verbs in the present

Most French verbs end in -er. Learn the pattern once and you can use hundreds of verbs.⁠​​‌​‌​​​​‌‌​​​‌‌​​‌​‌​​‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌‌‌​​‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌‌​‌​​​‌‌‌​​‌‌​‌‌​​​​‌​‌‌‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌⁠ Grade 6 French.

Learn what you need to know

How it works: take the verb (parler = to speak), remove -er → parl-, then add the ending for each person.

📝je parle, tu parles, il/elle parle, nous parlons, vous parlez, ils/elles parlent
⚽jouer = to play
🎵écouter = to listen
👀regarder = to watch
🏠habiter = to live
❤️aimer = to like
🍽️manger = to eat (nous mangeons)

Sound tip: je parle, tu parles, il parle and ils parlent all sound the same! You don't hear -s or -ent.

Key words

  • le verbe: verb
  • parler: to speak
  • jouer: to play
  • regarder: to watch
