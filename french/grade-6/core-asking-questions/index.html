Core French: Asking questions

Ask questions in French with est-ce que and question words: qui, où, quand, comment, pourquoi.⁠​​‌​‌​​​​‌‌​​​‌‌​​‌​‌​​‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌‌‌​​‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌‌​‌​​​‌‌‌​​‌‌​‌‌​​​​‌​‌‌‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌⁠ Grade 6 French.

Learn what you need to know

❓Yes/no question: put Est-ce que in front. Tu aimes le hockey. → Est-ce que tu aimes le hockey?
🗣️Or just raise your voice: Tu aimes le hockey?
🧑Qui? = Who?
📍Où? = Where?
📅Quand? = When?
🛠️Comment? = How?
🤔Pourquoi? = Why? → Parce que… = Because…
🔢Combien? = How many / how much?
❔Qu'est-ce que…? = What…?

Pattern: question word + est-ce que + sentence: Où est-ce que tu habites? (Where do you live?)

Key words

  • qui: who
  • où: where
  • quand: when
  • pourquoi: why
