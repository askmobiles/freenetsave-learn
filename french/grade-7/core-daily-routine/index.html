Core French: My daily routine

Describe your day in order with simple routine verbs and time words: d'abord, ensuite, puis, finalement.⁠​​‌​‌​​​​‌‌​​​‌‌​​‌​‌​​‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌‌‌​​‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌‌​‌​​​‌‌‌​​‌‌​‌‌​​​​‌​‌‌‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌⁠ Grade 7 French.

Learn what you need to know

⏰je me réveille = I wake up
🛏️je me lève = I get up
🚿je me douche = I take a shower
🪥je me brosse les dents = I brush my teeth
👕je m'habille = I get dressed
😴je me couche = I go to bed
🔢Order words: d'abord (first), ensuite (next), puis (then), après (after), finalement (finally)
🕗à sept heures (at seven), le matin, l'après-midi, le soir

Reflexive verbs have a little extra word: me (myself). Before a vowel it becomes m': je m'habille.

Key words

  • la routine: routine
  • d'abord: first
  • ensuite: next
  • finalement: finally
