Core French: My, your, his and her

Say whose things they are with mon, ma, mes, ton, ta, tes, son, sa, ses.⁠​​‌​‌​​​​‌‌​​​‌‌​​‌​‌​​‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌‌‌​​‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌‌​‌​​​‌‌‌​​‌‌​‌‌​​​​‌​‌‌‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌⁠ Grade 7 French.

Learn what you need to know

🙋my: mon (masc.), ma (fem.), mes (plural)
👉your: ton, ta, tes
👤his OR her: son, sa, ses
📘mon livre, ta règle, ses crayons
⚠️Before a vowel use mon/ton/son even for feminine words: mon amie, ton école

Big difference from English: son/sa match the THING, not the owner. « sa mère » can mean his mother OR her mother.

Key words

  • mon / ma / mes: my
  • ton / ta / tes: your
  • son / sa / ses: his / her
  • le propriétaire: owner
