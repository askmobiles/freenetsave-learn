Learn what you need to know

Every media text is constructed: someone chose the headline, the photos, the sources and what to leave out. This choice is called framing.

📰News report: aims to inform with facts and sources
🗣️Opinion/editorial: argues a viewpoint
💰Sponsored content: paid by an advertiser, sometimes made to look like news
🤖AI-generated content: text, images or video created by software, sometimes without labels
🔍Lateral reading: open new tabs and check what trusted sources say about the site and claim
🧭SIFT: Stop, Investigate the source, Find better coverage, Trace claims to the original
📈Algorithms show you more of what you engage with, creating "filter bubbles"
🧪Check images: reverse image search, look for odd details and missing context

Ask: Who made this? How do they know? What is missing? Who benefits if I believe and share it?

Key words

  • framing: how choices in a media text shape the audience's understanding
  • filter bubble: a personal information space created by algorithms showing similar content
  • lateral reading: checking a source by reading what other trusted sources say about it
  • SIFT: Stop, Investigate, Find better coverage, Trace claims
