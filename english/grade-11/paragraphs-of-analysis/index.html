Paragraphs of analysis

Learn what you need to know

An analytical paragraph proves one claim about a text. In senior English, the explanation matters more than the quotation.

1️⃣Claim (topic sentence): an arguable point about HOW or WHY the text works
2️⃣Context: briefly set up the quotation (who, when)
3️⃣Evidence: a short, integrated quotation
4️⃣Analysis: zoom in on specific words or techniques and explain their effect
5️⃣Link: connect back to the thesis or bigger theme

Zoom in: instead of "This quote shows he is sad," write "The word 'hollow' suggests he feels emptied of purpose, not just sad."

Aim for about two sentences of analysis for every sentence of evidence.

Avoid: plot summary, dropped quotations, and vague verbs like "shows" over and over. Try: suggests, reveals, emphasizes, conveys, implies, highlights.

Key words

  • claim: an arguable point the paragraph proves
  • integrated quotation: a quotation woven into your own sentence
  • close reading: careful analysis of specific words and techniques
  • commentary: your explanation of how evidence supports your claim
