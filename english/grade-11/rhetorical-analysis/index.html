Rhetorical analysis

Learn what you need to know

A rhetorical analysis explains HOW a text persuades — not whether you agree with it.

🌍Context: when, where and why was it created?
👥Audience: who is it for?
🎯Purpose: what does the speaker want the audience to think, feel or do?
🗣️Speaker: who is speaking, and why should we trust them? (ethos)
🎓Ethos: credibility
❤️Pathos: emotion
🧠Logos: logic and evidence
⏳Kairos: the right moment — why this message now?

Techniques to look for: repetition, anaphora, rhetorical questions, antithesis, inclusive language ("we"), anecdotes, statistics, imagery, tone shifts.

A strong analysis paragraph: claim about a technique → quotation → explanation of the effect on the audience → link to purpose.

Avoid summary. Don't retell what the speaker said; explain how and why it works.

Key words

  • rhetorical analysis: an explanation of how a text persuades its audience
  • kairos: the timeliness or right moment of a message
  • purpose: what the speaker wants the audience to think, feel or do
  • anecdote: a short personal story used to make a point
