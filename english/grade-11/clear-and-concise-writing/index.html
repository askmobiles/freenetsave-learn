Clear and concise writing

Learn what you need to know

Concise writing uses no more words than needed. It is not the same as short writing: every word should do a job.

✂️Cut filler: "due to the fact that" → "because"; "at this point in time" → "now"; "in order to" → "to"
🔁Cut redundancy: "past history", "end result", "free gift", "each and every"
💪Use strong verbs: "made a decision" → "decided"; "gave an explanation" → "explained"
🎯Prefer active voice: "The committee approved the plan" instead of "The plan was approved by the committee"
🚫Avoid empty openers: "There are many students who…" → "Many students…"

Turn nominalizations (verbs hidden in nouns) back into verbs: "The implementation of the policy" → "Implementing the policy" or "When the school implemented the policy…"

Test: read each sentence and ask, "Can I say this in fewer words without losing meaning?"

Key words

  • concise: expressing much in few words
  • redundancy: saying the same thing twice
  • nominalization: a verb or adjective turned into a noun, like "decision" from "decide"
  • active voice: the subject performs the action
