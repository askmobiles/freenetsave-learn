Learn what you need to know

Revising changes ideas and structure. Editing improves sentences and word choice. Proofreading catches small errors — spelling, punctuation, typos — at the very end.

1️⃣Big picture: Does every paragraph support the thesis? Is the order logical?
2️⃣Paragraphs: clear topic sentences, evidence and analysis, transitions
3️⃣Sentences: fragments, run-ons, comma splices, agreement, tense
4️⃣Words: repetition, vague words, wrong words (their/there, affect/effect)
5️⃣Format: quotations, citations, title, page numbers

Proofreading tricks: read aloud; read backwards sentence by sentence; print it; change the font; wait a day.

Spell-check misses real words in the wrong place — "form" for "from", "their" for "there".

Key words

  • revising: improving ideas, structure and content
  • editing: improving sentences, clarity and word choice
  • proofreading: final check for small errors
  • typo: a small typing mistake
