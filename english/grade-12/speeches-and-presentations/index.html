Learn what you need to know

🪝Hook: a question, story, surprising fact or quotation
🎯Clear purpose and thesis stated early
3️⃣Three main points, each with evidence and an example
🔁Signposting: "First…", "My second point…", "Finally…"
📣Strong ending: a call to action, memorable line or return to the hook
👀Eye contact with different parts of the audience
🔊Volume, pace and pauses — slow down for key points
🙌Natural gestures and open posture
🗂️Use cue cards with key words, not full sentences
🖼️Slides: few words, large images, one idea per slide

Writing for the ear: use short sentences, repetition and the rule of three — listeners cannot reread.

Key words

  • hook: an opening that grabs attention
  • signposting: phrases that tell listeners where the speech is going
  • call to action: asking the audience to do something
  • rule of three: grouping ideas in threes for impact and memory
