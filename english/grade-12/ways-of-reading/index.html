Learn what you need to know

A critical lens is a way of reading that asks a specific set of questions. Different lenses can reveal different meanings in the same text.

🔍Formalist: How do the form and techniques (imagery, structure, symbols) create meaning?
🧠Psychological: What drives the characters' fears, desires and choices?
🏛️Historical: How does the time and place the text was written in shape it?
⚖️Feminist / gender: How are gender roles shown, and who has power?
💰Marxist / economic: How do class and money shape characters' lives?
🌍Postcolonial: How are colonized peoples and cultures represented, and whose voices are heard?
🌱Ecocritical: How does the text show nature and humanity's relationship to it?
📖Reader-response: How does the reader's own experience shape the meaning?

No lens is "right". A strong essay picks a lens and supports its reading with evidence from the text.

Key words

  • critical lens: a way of reading that focuses on particular questions
  • formalist: focused on the text's structure and techniques
  • postcolonial: focused on the effects of colonization and whose voices are heard
  • reader-response: focused on how readers create meaning
