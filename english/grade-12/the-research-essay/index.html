The research essay

Learn what you need to know

❓Choose a focused research question: "How did the residential school system affect Indigenous languages in Canada?"
📚Find credible sources: academic journals, government data, reputable news, books from experts
📝Take notes with source details for every fact or quotation
🎯Write a thesis that answers your question and can be argued
🧱Outline: introduction, body paragraphs (one point each), counterargument, conclusion
🔗Integrate and cite sources (MLA or APA) and add a Works Cited or References list

Synthesis means combining several sources to build your own argument — not just reporting what each source says.

Your voice leads. Sources support your argument; they don't replace it.

Key words

  • research question: the focused question your essay answers
  • credible source: a reliable source from an expert or reputable organization
  • synthesis: combining sources to support your own argument
  • Works Cited: the list of sources at the end of an MLA essay
