Learn what you need to know

Style is how something is written: word choice (diction), sentence length and structure (syntax), and tone. Voice is the personality the reader hears in the writing.

🎩Formal: no contractions or slang, precise vocabulary, third person — essays, reports, applications
👕Informal: contractions, everyday words, first and second person — texts, blogs, friendly emails
🎯Audience: who will read it, and what they already know
🧭Purpose: to inform, persuade, entertain or reflect

Sentence variety: a short sentence after several long ones creates emphasis. Long, flowing sentences can build suspense or show complex thinking.

Register means the level of formality. The same idea: "The proposal lacks supporting data" (formal) vs "There's no proof this will work" (informal).

Ask before writing: Who is reading? Why? What tone will earn their trust?

Key words

  • diction: word choice
  • syntax: the arrangement of words and sentence structure
  • register: the level of formality in language
  • voice: the personality a reader hears in writing
