Learn what you need to know

🗺️Allegory: a story in which characters and events stand for bigger ideas
🔁Juxtaposition: placing two contrasting things side by side
🎭Dramatic irony: the audience knows something a character does not
😏Satire: humour or exaggeration used to criticize
🌫️Ambiguity: something that can be understood in more than one way
📜Archetype: a universal character or pattern — the mentor, the journey
🪞Foil: a character who contrasts with another to highlight their traits
🎬Verisimilitude: the appearance of being true or real

Also useful: motif (a repeated element), pathetic fallacy (weather reflecting mood), epiphany (a sudden realization), denouement (the final unravelling after the climax).

Naming is not enough. Always explain what the device does in the text.

Key words

  • allegory: a story where characters and events represent bigger ideas
  • juxtaposition: placing contrasting things side by side
  • foil: a character who highlights another's traits by contrast
  • epiphany: a sudden moment of realization
