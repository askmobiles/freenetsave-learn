Learn what you need to know

👁️First person: "I" — close, but limited and possibly biased
🌐Third-person omniscient: the narrator knows every character's thoughts
🔦Third-person limited: follows one character's thoughts
🌀Stream of consciousness: the flow of a character's thoughts, often without clear order
🎭Unreliable narrator: a narrator whose account we cannot fully trust
⏪Non-linear structure: events out of time order, using flashbacks
🖼️Frame story: a story told inside another story
✉️Epistolary: a story told through letters, diaries or messages
🔮Foreshadowing: hints of what will happen later
🔄In medias res: starting in the middle of the action

Ask: Why did the writer choose this narrator and this structure? What would change if the story were told another way?

Key words

  • unreliable narrator: a narrator whose account cannot be fully trusted
  • frame story: a story that contains another story
  • epistolary: told through letters, diaries or messages
  • in medias res: beginning in the middle of the action
