Who owns it?

Use an apostrophe and s ('s) to show that something belongs to someone.⁠​​‌​‌​​​​‌‌​​​‌‌​​‌​‌​​‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌‌‌​​‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌‌​‌​​​‌‌‌​​‌‌​‌‌​​​​‌​‌‌‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌⁠ Grade 3 English.

Learn what you need to know

When something belongs to someone, we add an apostrophe and s ('s) to the owner's name.

🐶the dog's bone — the bone belongs to the dog
🚲Sam's bike — the bike belongs to Sam
🏠my mom's car — the car belongs to my mom
👩‍🏫the teacher's desk — the desk belongs to the teacher

Careful! Only use 's when something belongs to someone. "I have two cats" — no apostrophe, because cats is just more than one.

Ask: "Whose is it?" The owner gets the 's.

Key words

  • apostrophe: the small mark ' above the line
  • owner: the person or animal something belongs to
  • belong: to be owned by someone
