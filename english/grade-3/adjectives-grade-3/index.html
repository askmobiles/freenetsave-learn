Adjectives

Adjectives are describing words. They tell what kind, how many and which one.⁠​​‌​‌​​​​‌‌​​​‌‌​​‌​‌​​‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌‌‌​​‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌‌​‌​​​‌‌‌​​‌‌​‌‌​​​​‌​‌‌‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌⁠ Grade 3 English.

Learn what you need to know

An adjective describes a noun (a person, place or thing). It makes writing more interesting!

🎨What kind? a red ball, a fluffy cat, a cold day
🔢How many? three apples, many birds, some cookies
👆Which one? this book, that hat, the last cookie
😊Feelings: a happy girl, a tired dog, a scared mouse
👃Senses: a loud drum, a sweet smell, a bumpy road

Compare: "I saw a dog." → "I saw a big, fluffy, brown dog." Which one paints a better picture?

Find it: ask "What kind?", "How many?" or "Which one?" about the noun.

Key words

  • adjective: a word that describes a noun
  • noun: a person, place or thing
  • describe: to tell what something is like
