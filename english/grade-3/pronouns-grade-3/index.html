Pronouns

Pronouns are short words like he, she, it and they that take the place of names.⁠​​‌​‌​​​​‌‌​​​‌‌​​‌​‌​​‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌‌‌​​‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌‌​‌​​​‌‌‌​​‌‌​‌‌​​​​‌​‌‌‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌⁠ Grade 3 English.

Learn what you need to know

A pronoun takes the place of a noun so we don't have to say the same name again and again.

🙋I, me — the person talking
👉you — the person you are talking to
👦he, him — a boy or man
👧she, her — a girl or woman
🐶it — a thing or an animal
👫we, us — me and others
👨‍👩‍👧they, them — more than one person or thing

Without pronouns: "Sam likes Sam's dog. Sam walks Sam's dog." With pronouns: "Sam likes his dog. He walks it."

Tip: say your name last — "Mia and I played," not "I and Mia."

Key words

  • pronoun: a word that takes the place of a noun
  • noun: a person, place or thing
  • replace: to put something in the place of something else
