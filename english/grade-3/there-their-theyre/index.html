there, their, they're

Three words that sound the same: there (a place), their (belongs to them) and they're (they are).⁠​​‌​‌​​​​‌‌​​​‌‌​​‌​‌​​‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌‌‌​​‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌‌​‌​​​‌‌‌​​‌‌​‌‌​​​​‌​‌‌‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌⁠ Grade 3 English.

Learn what you need to know

📍there: a place — "Put the box over there." Also "There is a cat."
🎒their: belongs to them — "The kids took their backpacks."
🤝they're: short for they are — "They're going to the park."

Tricks: there has the word here inside it (both are places). Their has i — like a person owning something. They're: try saying "they are" — if it fits, use they're.

Check they're: "They're happy" = "They are happy" ✔️

Key words

  • contraction: two words joined with an apostrophe, like they're
  • apostrophe: the mark ' that shows letters are missing
  • belong: to be owned by someone
