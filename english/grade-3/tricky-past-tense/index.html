Tricky past tense

Most verbs add -ed for the past. Some verbs change in a special way.⁠​​‌​‌​​​​‌‌​​​‌‌​​‌​‌​​‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌‌‌​​‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌‌​‌​​​‌‌‌​​‌‌​‌‌​​​​‌​‌‌‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌⁠ Grade 3 English.

Learn what you need to know

The past tense tells about something that already happened. Most verbs add -ed: jump → jumped, play → played.

🏃run → ran
🍎eat → ate
🚶go → went
👀see → saw
🎁give → gave
🎶sing → sang
🛏️sleep → slept
🏊swim → swam
✏️make → made
🖐️have → had

Listen for it: we say "I went to the park," not "I goed."

Key words

  • past tense: something that already happened
  • verb: an action word
  • irregular: does not follow the -ed rule

See real-life examples

Story time

"Once upon a time, a fox ran into the woods."

Weekend news

"On Saturday I went swimming and ate pizza."

Little kids

Small children often say "I runned" before they learn "I ran."
