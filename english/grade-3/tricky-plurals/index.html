Tricky plurals

Most words add -s to mean more than one. Some words change in other ways!⁠​​‌​‌​​​​‌‌​​​‌‌​​‌​‌​​‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌‌‌​​‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌‌​‌​​​‌‌‌​​‌‌​‌‌​​​​‌​‌‌‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌⁠ Grade 3 English.

Learn what you need to know

Plural means more than one. Most words just add -s: cat → cats, book → books.

📦Add -es after s, x, ch, sh: box → boxes, bus → buses, lunch → lunches, dish → dishes
🍒Consonant + y: change y to i and add -es: cherry → cherries, puppy → puppies
🔑Vowel + y: just add -s: key → keys, toy → toys
🍃Some f words change to -ves: leaf → leaves, wolf → wolves, knife → knives
🦷Special words: child → children, mouse → mice, tooth → teeth, foot → feet, man → men
🐑Same for one or many: sheep → sheep, deer → deer, fish → fish

Tip: say the word out loud. If you hear an extra sound like "box-es", add -es.

Key words

  • plural: more than one
  • singular: just one
  • vowel: the letters a, e, i, o, u
