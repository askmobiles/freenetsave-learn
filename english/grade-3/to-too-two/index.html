to, too, two

Three words that sound the same but mean different things: to, too and two.⁠​​‌​‌​​​​‌‌​​​‌‌​​‌​‌​​‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌‌‌​​‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌‌​‌​​​‌‌‌​​‌‌​‌‌​​​​‌​‌‌‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌⁠ Grade 3 English.

Learn what you need to know

➡️to: shows where or what — "We went to the park." "I like to draw."
➕too: means "also" or "more than enough" — "I want to come too!" "It is too hot."
2️⃣two: the number 2 — "I have two cats."

Memory trick: too has an extra o — it has "too many" o's! Two has a w like in "twins" and "twelve."

Check: can you put "also" or "very much" in its place? Then use too.

Key words

  • homophones: words that sound the same but have different spellings and meanings
  • also: in addition, as well
  • number: a word or symbol that shows how many
