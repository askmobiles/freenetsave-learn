Capitals for names

Names of people, places, days, months and holidays start with a capital letter.⁠​​‌​‌​​​​‌‌​​​‌‌​​‌​‌​​‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌‌‌​​‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌‌​‌​​​‌‌‌​​‌‌​‌‌​​​​‌​‌‌‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌⁠ Grade 3 English.

Learn what you need to know

A capital letter (A, B, C...) is used at the start of every sentence and for special names.

👤People and pets: Maya, Mr. Lee, Buddy
🏙️Places: Toronto, Canada, Lake Ontario, Main Street
📅Days and months: Monday, Friday, January, July
🎉Holidays: Canada Day, Diwali, Christmas, Eid
📚Titles: The Cat in the Hat (big words in a title)
🙋The word I is always a capital

No capital for seasons (spring, summer) or for general words (a city, my teacher, a dog).

Ask: is it a special name? Then use a capital letter.

Key words

  • capital letter: a big letter like A, B, C
  • proper noun: the special name of a person, place or thing
  • season: spring, summer, fall or winter
