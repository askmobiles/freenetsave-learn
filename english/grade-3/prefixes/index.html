Prefixes

A prefix is a small part added to the start of a word that changes its meaning: un-, re-, pre- and dis-.⁠​​‌​‌​​​​‌‌​​​‌‌​​‌​‌​​‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌‌‌​​‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌‌​‌​​​‌‌‌​​‌‌​‌‌​​​​‌​‌‌‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌⁠ Grade 3 English.

Learn what you need to know

A prefix goes at the beginning of a word and changes what it means.

🚫un- = not: unhappy (not happy), unlock, unfair
🔁re- = again: redo (do again), reread, refill
⏮️pre- = before: preview (see before), preheat, preschool
↩️dis- = not or opposite: dislike, disagree, disappear
❌mis- = wrongly: misspell (spell wrongly), mistake

Word detective: cover the prefix, read the base word, then add the prefix meaning back. re + play = play again.

Key words

  • prefix: a word part added to the beginning of a word
  • base word: the main word before a prefix or suffix is added
  • meaning: what a word tells us
