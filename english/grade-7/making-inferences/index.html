Making inferences

Learn what you need to know

An inference is a smart guess based on evidence from the text + what you already know.

🔍Clue from the text: "Mia slammed the door and threw her bag on the floor."
🧠What I know: people slam doors when they are upset
💡Inference: Mia is angry or frustrated

Writers often show instead of tell. They give clues about feelings, setting, time and what will happen next.

Prove it: a good inference can always be backed up with a clue from the text.

Key words

  • inference: a conclusion based on clues and what you already know
  • evidence: details from the text that support an idea
  • prior knowledge: what you already know
  • implied: suggested but not said directly
