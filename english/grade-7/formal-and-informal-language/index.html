Learn what you need to know

🎩Formal: full words, no slang, polite and serious — letters to principals, essays, job applications
👕Informal: contractions, slang, friendly — texts to friends, chats, notes to family
🎯Audience: who will read or hear it?
🧭Purpose: why are you writing — to ask, thank, explain, persuade?

Compare: informal "Hey, can u send me the notes? thx!" vs formal "Hello Ms. Singh, could you please send me the notes from today's class? Thank you."

Ask: Would I say it this way to the principal? If not, make it more formal.

Key words

  • formal language: serious, polite language without slang
  • informal language: casual, friendly everyday language
  • slang: very casual words used by certain groups, like "lit" or "sus"
  • audience: the people who will read or hear your words
