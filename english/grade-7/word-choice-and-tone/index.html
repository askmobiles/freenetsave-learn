Word choice and tone

Learn what you need to know

Tone is the writer's attitude toward the subject (serious, funny, angry, hopeful). Mood is the feeling the reader gets (scared, calm, excited).

😊Positive words: delighted, sparkling, cozy, brave
😐Neutral words: said, walked, house, small
😟Negative words: snapped, trudged, shack, tiny and cramped
🎯Precise words: "sprinted" says more than "ran fast"

Compare: "The puppy bounced into the room" (happy) vs "The dog crept into the room" (tense). One word changes the feeling.

Tone words: cheerful, sarcastic, serious, angry, hopeful, gloomy, playful, worried.

Key words

  • tone: the writer's attitude toward the subject
  • mood: the feeling the reader gets
  • connotation: the feeling a word carries
  • precise word: a word that says exactly what you mean

See real-life examples

Movie trailers

Music and word choice set a scary or funny mood.

Text messages

Reviews
