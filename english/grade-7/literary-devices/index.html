Learn what you need to know

≈Simile: compares using like or as — "Her voice was as soft as snow."
=Metaphor: says one thing IS another — "The classroom was a zoo."
🗣️Personification: gives human qualities to things — "The wind whispered."
📈Hyperbole: huge exaggeration — "I have a million chores."
💥Onomatopoeia: words that sound like their meaning — buzz, crash, sizzle
🔤Alliteration: repeated first sounds — "Silly snakes slide slowly."
🎨Imagery: words that appeal to the five senses

Ask why: don't just name the device — explain the picture or feeling it creates. "The wind whispered" makes the night feel quiet and secret.

Key words

  • simile: a comparison using like or as
  • metaphor: a comparison saying one thing is another
  • personification: giving human qualities to non-human things
  • hyperbole: extreme exaggeration for effect
