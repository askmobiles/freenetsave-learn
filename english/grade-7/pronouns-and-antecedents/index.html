Pronouns and antecedents

Make pronouns agree with the words they replace, and avoid confusing pronoun references.⁠​​‌​‌​​​​‌‌​​​‌‌​​‌​‌​​‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌‌‌​​‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌‌​‌​​​‌‌‌​​‌‌​‌‌​​​​‌​‌‌‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌⁠ Grade 7 English.

Learn what you need to know

An antecedent is the noun a pronoun refers to. In "Maria lost her keys", Maria is the antecedent of her.

1️⃣Agree in number: one thing → it, he, she; more than one → they, them
🧑Each, everyone, someone, nobody are singular — "Everyone brought their (or his or her) lunch" is common today
❓Clear reference: "When Sam met Leo, he was nervous." Who was nervous? Name the person instead.
🙋Use "I" and "me" correctly: "Ali and I went" / "Mom drove Ali and me"

Check: point to each pronoun and ask, "Who or what does this mean?" If you can't tell, rewrite.

Key words

  • pronoun: a word that replaces a noun, like he, she, it or they
  • antecedent: the noun a pronoun refers to
  • agreement: matching in number (one or more than one)
  • vague pronoun: a pronoun whose antecedent is unclear
