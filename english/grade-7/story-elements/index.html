Learn what you need to know

🏞️Setting: where and when the story happens
👤Characters: protagonist (main character) and antagonist (who or what works against them)
📈Plot: the events in order — beginning, rising action, climax, falling action, resolution
⚔️Conflict: the problem — person vs person, person vs self, person vs nature, person vs society
💡Theme: the message or lesson about life

Characterization is how a writer shows what a character is like: through what they say, do, think and how others react to them.

Theme is not one word. "Friendship" is a topic. "True friends stand by you when things are hard" is a theme.

Key words

  • protagonist: the main character
  • antagonist: the character or force that works against the main character
  • conflict: the main problem in a story
  • theme: the message or lesson about life
