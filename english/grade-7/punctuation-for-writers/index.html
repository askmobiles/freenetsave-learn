,Commas in a list: "We packed food, water, and a map."
,Comma after an introductory word or phrase: "First, we packed."
,Commas around extra information: "Mr. Lee, our coach, smiled."
“ ”Dialogue: "Let's go," said Ana. Punctuation goes inside the quotation marks.
↵New speaker, new paragraph
'Apostrophes for possession (Ali's book) and contractions (can't)

Dialogue tags: when the tag comes after, end the speech with a comma (not a period): "I'm hungry," Sam said. If the speech is a question or exclamation, keep the ? or !: "Are you coming?" asked Mom.

Read it aloud. Commas often go where you naturally pause.

Key words

  • dialogue: the words characters say to each other
  • dialogue tag: words that show who is speaking, like "she said"
  • introductory phrase: words that come before the main part of a sentence
  • apostrophe: a mark (') used for possession and contractions
