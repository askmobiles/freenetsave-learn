Learn what you need to know

When you find a word you don't know, the context (the words around it) often gives you a clue.

📖Definition clue: the meaning is right there — "The arid, or very dry, land had no trees."
🔁Synonym clue: a word with a similar meaning — "She was exhausted; she was so tired."
↔️Antonym clue: a word with the opposite meaning — "Unlike his timid sister, Max was brave."
🎯Example clue: examples show the meaning — "Reptiles, such as snakes and turtles, are cold-blooded."

Clue words: or, which means, like, such as, unlike, but, however.

Key words

  • context clue: a hint in the nearby words that helps you figure out a meaning
  • definition: the meaning of a word
  • synonym: a word with a similar meaning
  • antonym: a word with the opposite meaning
