Using commas

Commas in lists, after introductory words, in dates and addresses, and before joining words.⁠​​‌​‌​​​​‌‌​​​‌‌​​‌​‌​​‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌‌‌​​‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌‌​‌​​​‌‌‌​​‌‌​‌‌​​​​‌​‌‌‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌⁠ Grade 5 English.

Learn what you need to know

🛒Lists: "I bought apples, bread, and milk."
👋After introductory words: "Yes, I can come." "First, mix the flour."
📅Dates: "July 1, 1867" — between the day and the year
📍Places: "Halifax, Nova Scotia" — between a city and a province
🗣️Speaking to someone: "Sam, please close the door."
🔗Before and, but, so when joining two sentences: "It rained, so we stayed in."

Read it out loud. A comma often goes where you take a small breath.

Key words

  • comma: a punctuation mark (,) that shows a small pause or separates items
  • series: a list of three or more things
  • introductory word: a word like yes, no, first or well at the start of a sentence
  • direct address: speaking to someone by name
