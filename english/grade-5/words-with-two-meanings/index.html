Words with two meanings

Some words are spelled the same but mean different things. Use the rest of the sentence to choose the right meaning.⁠​​‌​‌​​​​‌‌​​​‌‌​​‌​‌​​‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌‌‌​​‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌‌​‌​​​‌‌‌​​‌‌​‌‌​​​​‌​‌‌‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌⁠ Grade 5 English.

Learn what you need to know

Words that are spelled the same but have different meanings are called multiple-meaning words (or homographs).

🦇bat: an animal that flies at night / a stick for hitting a ball
🏦bank: a place to keep money / the side of a river
🌊wave: water moving in the ocean / move your hand to say hi
⭐star: a light in the night sky / a famous person
🍊light: not heavy / something that helps you see
🪵bark: the sound a dog makes / the outside of a tree

Context clues: read the words around it. "The dog's bark was loud" — dogs bark, so it is the sound.

Key words

  • multiple-meaning word: a word with more than one meaning
  • homograph: a word spelled the same as another but with a different meaning
  • context: the words and ideas around a word
  • meaning: what a word tells us
