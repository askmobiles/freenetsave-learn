Adverbs

Adverbs tell how, when, where and how often something happens.⁠​​‌​‌​​​​‌‌​​​‌‌​​‌​‌​​‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌‌‌​​‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌‌​‌​​​‌‌‌​​‌‌​‌‌​​​​‌​‌‌‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌⁠ Grade 5 English.

Learn what you need to know

An adverb describes a verb, an adjective or another adverb. Many adverbs end in -ly.

🐢How: slowly, quickly, carefully, loudly, happily
🕒When: now, later, yesterday, soon, today
📍Where: here, there, outside, upstairs, everywhere
🔁How often: always, often, sometimes, never, daily
📈How much: very, too, quite, almost

Example: "The turtle moved slowly." Slowly tells how the turtle moved.

Careful: "good" is an adjective; "well" is the adverb. "She sings well," not "She sings good."

Key words

  • adverb: a word that describes a verb, adjective or another adverb
  • -ly: an ending that turns many adjectives into adverbs
  • frequency: how often something happens
  • adjective: a word that describes a noun
