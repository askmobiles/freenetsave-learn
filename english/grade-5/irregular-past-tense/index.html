Irregular past tense

Many common verbs don't add -ed in the past tense. Learn the tricky ones.⁠​​‌​‌​​​​‌‌​​​‌‌​​‌​‌​​‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌‌‌​​‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌‌​‌​​​‌‌‌​​‌‌​‌‌​​​​‌​‌‌‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌⁠ Grade 5 English.

Learn what you need to know

Regular verbs add -ed: walk → walked, jump → jumped. Irregular verbs change in other ways.

🏃run → ran
🍽️eat → ate
🚶go → went
👀see → saw
✍️write → wrote
🗣️say → said
🛒buy → bought
💭think → thought
🏊swim → swam
🎁bring → brought

Some verbs don't change at all: cut → cut, put → put, hit → hit.

Common mistakes: "goed", "runned", "bringed" — say went, ran, brought.

Key words

  • past tense: the verb form for something that already happened
  • regular verb: a verb that adds -ed in the past tense
  • irregular verb: a verb that changes in a different way in the past tense
  • verb: an action word
