Canadian spelling

Learn what you need to know

Canadian spelling is a mix of British and American spelling. Here are the patterns used in most Canadian schools and newspapers.

🎨-our: colour, favourite, neighbour, honour, behaviour
🏙️-re: centre, theatre, metre, litre, fibre
✈️Double L: travelled, cancelled, jewellery, marvellous
📄cheque (for money), not check
🔤Words with -ize are usual in Canada: organize, realize, recognize
🚗Like Americans, we write tire, curb and program

Practice / practise: many Canadians use practice as a noun ("hockey practice") and practise as a verb ("I practise piano"). Licence / license works the same way.

Remember: in Canada, colour has a "u" and centre ends in "re".

Key words

  • spelling pattern: a group of letters that repeats in many words
  • Canadian spelling: the way words are usually spelled in Canada
  • noun: a person, place, thing or idea
  • verb: an action word
