Learn what you need to know

📑Table of contents: lists chapters and page numbers at the front
🔠Heading: tells what a section is about
🖼️Photo and caption: a picture with words that explain it
🔤Bold words: important words to notice
📖Glossary: a small dictionary at the back of the book
🗂️Index: an A–Z list of topics and their page numbers at the back
🗺️Diagram, map, chart: pictures that show information
💬Sidebar: a box with extra facts

Before reading: scan the headings, pictures and bold words to preview what you will learn.

Key words

  • text feature: a part of a text that helps you find or understand information
  • caption: words under a picture that explain it
  • glossary: a list of important words and meanings at the back of a book
  • index: an A–Z list of topics and page numbers
