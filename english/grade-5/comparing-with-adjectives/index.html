Comparing with adjectives

Compare things with -er and -est, more and most, and the special words good, better, best.⁠​​‌​‌​​​​‌‌​​​‌‌​​‌​‌​​‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌‌‌​​‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌‌​‌​​​‌‌‌​​‌‌​‌‌​​​​‌​‌‌‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌⁠ Grade 5 English.

Learn what you need to know

2️⃣Comparing two things: add -er or use "more" — taller, more colourful
3️⃣Comparing three or more: add -est or use "most" — tallest, most colourful
📏Short words: add -er / -est — fast, faster, fastest
📚Long words: use more / most — more beautiful, most beautiful
⭐Special words: good, better, best · bad, worse, worst · far, farther, farthest

Spelling rules: big → bigger (double the last letter); happy → happier (y → i); nice → nicer (just add -r).

Never use both: not "more taller" — just "taller".

Key words

  • comparative: the form used to compare two things (-er, more)
  • superlative: the form used to compare three or more things (-est, most)
  • adjective: a word that describes a noun
  • irregular: not following the usual pattern
