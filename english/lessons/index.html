English lessons

Full lessons that teach each topic step by step, with pictures, real-life examples, practice, a game, a test and a challenge. Lessons for Grades 1 to 8 can be read aloud by a helper, and every lesson can be printed as a worksheet with an answer key.

Each grade follows the Ontario curriculum (📘) and adds ⭐ extra topics taught in other provinces and countries, so students, parents and teachers can choose what to learn or teach.

  2. 8Grade 8 English13 lessons: Verbals, Agreement and pronouns, Comma rules, Semicolons and colons and more
  3. 9Grade 9 English10 lessons: Parallel structure and modifiers, Sentence errors, Who and whom, lie and lay, Characters and narrators and more
  4. 10Grade 10 English10 lessons: Agreement: the hard cases, Punctuating quotations, Words that get confused, Rhetorical devices and more
