Telling, asking and shouting

Sentences can tell, ask or show strong feeling. Each one ends with its own mark: . ? !⁠​​‌​‌​​​​‌‌​​​‌‌​​‌​‌​​‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌‌‌​​‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌‌​‌​​​‌‌‌​​‌‌​‌‌​​​​‌​‌‌‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌⁠ Grade 2 English.

Learn what you need to know

.Telling sentence: tells something — ends with a period. "I like dogs."
?Asking sentence: asks a question — ends with a question mark. "Do you like dogs?"
!Shouting (exclaiming) sentence: shows strong feeling — ends with an exclamation mark. "I love dogs!"

Asking sentences often start with who, what, where, when, why, how, can, do or is.

Every sentence starts with a capital letter and ends with . ? or !

Key words

  • period: the mark . at the end of a telling sentence
  • question mark: the mark ? at the end of an asking sentence
  • exclamation mark: the mark ! that shows strong feeling
