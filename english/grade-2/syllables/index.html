Learn what you need to know

A syllable is a beat or part of a word. Every syllable has a vowel sound.

👏1 clap: cat, dog, sun, jump
👏👏2 claps: ta-ble, pen-cil, ap-ple, bas-ket
👏👏👏3 claps: but-ter-fly, el-e-phant, ba-na-na
👏👏👏👏4 claps: wa-ter-mel-on, al-li-ga-tor

Chin trick: put your hand under your chin and say the word. Each time your chin drops is one syllable!

Key words

  • syllable: a beat or part of a word
  • clap: to hit your hands together
  • beat: one part of a rhythm

See real-life examples

Music

Songs have beats like words have syllables.

Names

Clap your name: Ma-ya (2), Ben (1), Sa-man-tha (3).

Poems
