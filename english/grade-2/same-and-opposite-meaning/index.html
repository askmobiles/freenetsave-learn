Same meaning

Some words mean the same (big and large). Some words mean the opposite (hot and cold).⁠​​‌​‌​​​​‌‌​​​‌‌​​‌​‌​​‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌‌‌​​‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌‌​‌​​​‌‌‌​​‌‌​‌‌​​​​‌​‌‌‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌⁠ Grade 2 English.

Learn what you need to know

🟰Same meaning (synonyms): big = large, happy = glad, fast = quick, small = little
↔️Opposite meaning (antonyms): hot ↔ cold, up ↔ down, happy ↔ sad, open ↔ closed

Using a word with the same meaning makes your writing more interesting. Instead of "big, big, big," try "large" or "huge"!

Tip: Same = Synonym. Opposite = Antonym (like "against").

Key words

  • synonym: a word that means the same or almost the same
  • antonym: a word that means the opposite
  • opposite: as different as can be, like up and down
