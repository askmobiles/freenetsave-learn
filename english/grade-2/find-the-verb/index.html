Learn what you need to know

A verb is an action word. It tells what someone does.

🏃run
🦘jump
🍪eat
🎵sing
😴sleep
🏊swim
✍️write
👏clap

In "The frog jumps," the verb is jumps. It tells what the frog does.

Tip: ask "What does it do?" The answer is the verb.

Key words

  • verb: an action word
  • action: something you do
  • sentence: a group of words that tells a full idea
