Learn what you need to know

A compound word is made of two small words joined together.

🌻sun + flower = sunflower
⛄snow + man = snowman
🦋butter + fly = butterfly
🏈foot + ball = football
🌈rain + bow = rainbow
🧁cup + cake = cupcake

Tip: cover half of the word. Can you read two small words? Then it is a compound word!

Key words

  • compound word: two small words joined to make one word
  • join: to put together
  • small word: a short word inside a bigger word

See real-life examples

Winter

Snowman, snowball and snowflake are compound words.

Kitchen

Cupcake, pancake and teapot are compound words.

School

Classroom, backpack and playground are compound words.
