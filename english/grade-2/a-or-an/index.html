a or an

Use "a" before a consonant sound and "an" before a vowel sound: a cat, an apple.⁠​​‌​‌​​​​‌‌​​​‌‌​​‌​‌​​‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌‌‌​​‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌‌​‌​​​‌‌‌​​‌‌​‌‌​​​​‌​‌‌‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌⁠ Grade 2 English.

Learn what you need to know

We use a or an before a noun.

🐱a + consonant sound: a cat, a dog, a bus, a tree
🍎an + vowel sound (a, e, i, o, u): an apple, an egg, an ice cream, an owl, an umbrella

Listen to the first sound: an apple, an elephant, an orange.

Say it out loud. "An apple" is easy to say. "A apple" is hard to say!

Key words

  • vowel: the letters a, e, i, o, u
  • consonant: all the other letters, like b, c, d, m, t
  • sound: what you hear when you say a letter
