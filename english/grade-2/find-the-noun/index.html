Learn what you need to know

A noun is a naming word.

👩A person: mom, teacher, baby, Sam
🏫A place: school, park, home, Canada
🐻An animal: bear, cat, fish, bird
⚽A thing: ball, book, cup, bike

In "The dog ran to the park," the nouns are dog and park.

Tip: you can often put "a" or "the" in front of a noun: a cup, the park.

Key words

  • noun: a naming word
  • person: a man, woman, boy or girl
  • place: where something is
