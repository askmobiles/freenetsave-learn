is or are

Use "is" for one and "are" for more than one: The dog is happy. The dogs are happy.⁠​​‌​‌​​​​‌‌​​​‌‌​​‌​‌​​‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌‌‌​​‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌‌​‌​​​‌‌‌​​‌‌​‌‌​​​​‌​‌‌‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌⁠ Grade 2 English.

Learn what you need to know

1️⃣is = one: The cat is sleeping. The ball is red.
2️⃣are = more than one: The cats are sleeping. The balls are red.
👉You are... / We are... / They are...
🙋I am... (not "I is")

Tip: look at the naming word. Does it end in -s (more than one)? Use are.

Key words

  • one: just a single thing
  • more than one: two or more things
  • is / are: words that tell what something is like
