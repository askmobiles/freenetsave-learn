Contractions

A contraction joins two words into one short word with an apostrophe: do not → don't.⁠​​‌​‌​​​​‌‌​​​‌‌​​‌​‌​​‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌‌‌​​‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌‌​‌​​​‌‌‌​​‌‌​‌‌​​​​‌​‌‌‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌⁠ Grade 2 English.

Learn what you need to know

A contraction is two words squished together. An apostrophe (') takes the place of the missing letters.

🚫do not → don't
🙅can not → can't
👍it is → it's
🙋I am → I'm
👥we are → we're
📣you are → you're
🧍is not → isn't
🏃I will → I'll

Tip: the apostrophe goes where the letters are missing: do not → don't.

Key words

  • contraction: two words made into one short word
  • apostrophe: the mark ' that takes the place of missing letters
  • short: not long
