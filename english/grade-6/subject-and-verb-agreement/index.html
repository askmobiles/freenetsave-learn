Subject and verb agreement

Make the verb match its subject: one subject, one kind of verb; more than one, another.⁠​​‌​‌​​​​‌‌​​​‌‌​​‌​‌​​‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌‌‌​​‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌‌​‌​​​‌‌‌​​‌‌​‌‌​​​​‌​‌‌‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌⁠ Grade 6 English.

Learn what you need to know

1️⃣Singular subject → singular verb: "The dog barks."
2️⃣Plural subject → plural verb: "The dogs bark."
➕Two subjects joined by "and" are plural: "Sam and Ali play."
↔️With "or" / "nor," match the closer subject: "Neither the kids nor the teacher is ready."
🧍Each, everyone, somebody, nobody are singular: "Everyone is here."

Watch out for words in between: "The box of crayons is on the desk." The subject is box, not crayons.

Trick: cover the words between the subject and the verb, then check if they match.

Key words

  • subject: who or what the sentence is about
  • verb: the action or state of being
  • singular: one
  • plural: more than one
