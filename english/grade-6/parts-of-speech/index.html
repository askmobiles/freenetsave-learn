Parts of speech

Learn what you need to know

🏠Noun: a person, place, thing or idea — teacher, Halifax, kite, courage
🏃Verb: an action or state of being — jump, think, is
🎨Adjective: describes a noun — tall, shiny, three
⚡Adverb: describes a verb, adjective or another adverb — quickly, very, never
👉Pronoun: takes the place of a noun — she, it, they
📍Preposition: shows position or time — under, after, between
🔗Conjunction: joins words or ideas — and, but, because
❗Interjection: shows feeling — Wow! Oops!

A word's job can change. "I will paint the fence" (verb). "The paint is wet" (noun). Look at how the word is used in the sentence.

Key words

  • noun: a person, place, thing or idea
  • verb: an action or state of being
  • adjective: a word that describes a noun
  • adverb: a word that describes a verb, adjective or adverb
