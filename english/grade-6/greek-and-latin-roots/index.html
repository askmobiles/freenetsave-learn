Learn what you need to know

📞tele (far): telephone, telescope, television
🔭scope (look): microscope, telescope
✍️graph / scrib (write): autograph, paragraph, scribble
🌍geo (earth): geography, geology
🔬bio (life): biology, biography
🎵phon (sound): phone, microphone, symphony
🚗port (carry): transport, portable, export
👁️vis / vid (see): vision, video, visible
🔢micro (small): microscope, microwave
📜aud (hear): audio, audience, audible

Word detective: break a new word into parts. "Biography" = bio (life) + graph (write) → writing about someone's life.

Key words

  • root: the main part of a word that carries its meaning
  • prefix: a word part added to the beginning
  • suffix: a word part added to the end
  • origin: where a word comes from
