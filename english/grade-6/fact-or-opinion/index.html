Fact or opinion

Learn what you need to know

✅Fact: can be proven true or false — "Canada has ten provinces."
💭Opinion: tells what someone thinks or feels — "Winter is the best season."
🔍Opinion clue words: best, worst, should, beautiful, boring, I think, I believe
📊Facts can be checked with books, measurements or reliable websites

Many texts mix facts and opinions. An ad might say: "Our juice has 100% fruit (fact). It's the tastiest drink ever! (opinion)."

Ask: "Could I check this?" If yes, it's probably a fact. If people could disagree, it's an opinion.

Key words

  • fact: a statement that can be proven true or false
  • opinion: what someone thinks, feels or believes
  • evidence: information that supports an idea
  • reliable source: a source you can trust
