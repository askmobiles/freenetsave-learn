Direct and reported speech

Learn what you need to know

💬Direct speech: the exact words, in quotation marks — Ali said, "I am hungry."
📝Reported (indirect) speech: tells what someone said, no quotation marks — Ali said that he was hungry.
⏪In reported speech, verbs often move back in time: am → was, will → would, can → could
👉Pronouns change too: "I" → he/she; "my" → his/her

Direct speech: "We will win," said Coach. → Reported: Coach said that they would win.

Questions: "Are you coming?" asked Mom. → Mom asked if I was coming.

Key words

  • direct speech: the exact words someone says, in quotation marks
  • reported speech: telling what someone said in your own words
  • quotation marks: marks (" ") around exact words
  • reporting verb: a verb like said, asked or told
