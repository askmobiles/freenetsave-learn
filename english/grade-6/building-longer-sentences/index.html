Building longer sentences

Combine short, choppy sentences into longer, smoother ones using joining words, details and phrases.⁠​​‌​‌​​​​‌‌​​​‌‌​​‌​‌​​‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌‌‌​​‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌‌​‌​​​‌‌‌​​‌‌​‌‌​​​​‌​‌‌‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌⁠ Grade 6 English.

Learn what you need to know

Too many short sentences sound choppy: "I have a dog. Her name is Lulu. She is brown. She likes to run." We can combine them!

🔗Join with and, but, or, so: "Lulu is brown, and she likes to run."
🕰️Use when, because, after, although: "Lulu barks when the doorbell rings."
🎨Add describing words: "I have a brown dog named Lulu."
📍Add a phrase: "In the morning, Lulu runs around the park."
👤Use who, which, that: "I have a dog named Lulu, who loves to run."

Mix it up! Use some short sentences and some longer ones. A short sentence after long ones can be powerful.

Key words

  • combine: to join two or more things into one
  • choppy: made of many short, jerky sentences
  • joining word: a word like and, but, because or when
  • sentence variety: using different lengths and types of sentences
