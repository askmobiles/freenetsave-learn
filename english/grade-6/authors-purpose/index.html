Author's purpose

Learn what you need to know

🗣️Persuade: to convince you to think or do something — ads, letters to the editor, speeches
📚Inform: to teach facts — textbooks, news articles, encyclopedias
😄Entertain: to tell a story or make you laugh — novels, comics, poems
🛠️Explain or instruct: to show how to do something — recipes, manuals

Remember PIE: Persuade, Inform, Entertain. Some texts have more than one purpose, like a funny story that also teaches a lesson.

Clues: look at the title, the type of text, and the words the author uses (should, must, facts, jokes, steps).

Key words

  • author's purpose: the reason an author writes a text
  • persuade: to convince someone
  • inform: to give facts
  • entertain: to amuse or tell a story
