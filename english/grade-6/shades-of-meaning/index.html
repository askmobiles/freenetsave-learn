Shades of meaning

Words with similar meanings can be stronger or weaker. Choose the word that says exactly what you mean.⁠​​‌​‌​​​​‌‌​​​‌‌​​‌​‌​​‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌‌‌​​‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌‌​‌​​​‌‌‌​​‌‌​‌‌​​​​‌​‌‌‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌⁠ Grade 6 English.

Learn what you need to know

Many words mean almost the same thing but have different strengths. Putting them in order is called a word ladder or shades of meaning.

🌡️warm → hot → scorching
😊content → happy → thrilled → overjoyed
😠annoyed → angry → furious
🚶stroll → walk → hurry → sprint
🔉whisper → say → shout → bellow
🤏small → tiny → microscopic

Pick the precise word. "Furious" tells the reader much more than "mad."

Key words

  • synonym: a word with a similar meaning
  • intensity: how strong a word's meaning is
  • precise: exact and clear
  • nuance: a small difference in meaning

See real-life examples

Weather reports

"Chilly", "cold" and "frigid" tell you how to dress.

Stories

Authors choose "trembled" instead of "shook a little".

Reviews

"Good", "great" and "outstanding" show different opinions.
