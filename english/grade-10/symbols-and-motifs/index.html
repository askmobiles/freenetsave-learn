Symbols and motifs

Learn what you need to know

A symbol is something concrete — an object, colour, animal, place or action — that stands for an abstract idea.

🕊️Dove → peace
🌹Red rose → love or passion
🌱Spring → new beginnings
🌑Darkness → fear, evil or the unknown
🌊A river or road → a journey or change

Some symbols are universal (light for hope), others are cultural (in some cultures white is for weddings, in others for mourning), and some are created by an author within a single text.

A motif is an image, idea or phrase that repeats throughout a work and develops its theme. In Romeo and Juliet, light and dark imagery repeats: Romeo calls Juliet the sun, and the lovers meet at night.

An allegory is a whole story in which characters and events stand for ideas — for example, a farm story that represents a political revolution.

Spotting symbols: look for objects that are repeated, described in great detail, in the title, or present at key moments.

Key words

  • symbol: something concrete that represents an abstract idea
  • motif: a repeated image, idea or phrase that develops a theme
  • allegory: a story in which characters and events stand for ideas
  • abstract: an idea you cannot touch, like freedom or hope
