Advertising techniques

Learn what you need to know

Advertisers study their target audience — age, interests, income, values — and design every part of an ad to appeal to them: colours, music, models, slogans and where the ad appears.

⭐Celebrity endorsement
🐑Bandwagon: join everyone else
💎Snob appeal: be exclusive and elite
🏡Plain folks: ordinary, relatable people
😨Fear: what will happen if you do not buy
🔬Scientific claims: "clinically proven", lab coats, charts
🌟Glittering generalities: "the best", "natural", "amazing"
⏳Urgency: "Limited time!", "Only 3 left!"

Modern ads include influencer posts, sponsored content that looks like news, targeted ads based on your browsing data, and product placement in shows and games.

In Canada, the Competition Act bans false or misleading advertising, and paid influencer posts must be clearly disclosed. Quebec bans most advertising aimed at children under 13.

Critical consumer questions: Who paid for this? What are they really selling? What is left out? Do I need it?

Key words

  • target audience: the specific group an ad is designed to reach
  • slogan: a short, memorable phrase used in advertising
  • product placement: showing a brand inside a movie, show or game
  • targeted ad: an ad chosen for you based on your online data
