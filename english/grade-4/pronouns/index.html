Pronouns

Pronouns take the place of nouns so we do not repeat the same words.⁠​​‌​‌​​​​‌‌​​​‌‌​​‌​‌​​‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌‌‌​​‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌‌​‌​​​‌‌‌​​‌‌​‌‌​​​​‌​‌‌‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌⁠ Grade 4 English.

Learn what you need to know

A pronoun is a word that takes the place of a noun. Instead of saying "Sam lost Sam's hat," we say "Sam lost his hat."

🙋I, me, my
👉you, your
👦he, him, his
👧she, her, hers
📦it, its
👨‍👩‍👧we, us, they, them, their

Use I, he, she, we, they when the person is doing the action: She kicked the ball.

Use me, him, her, us, them when the action is done to the person: The ball hit him.

When you talk about yourself and someone else, put the other person first: Ali and I went home. The coach called Ali and me.

Trick: cover the other person's name. "Ali and me went" becomes "me went" — wrong! So say "Ali and I went."

Key words

  • pronoun: a word that takes the place of a noun
  • subject pronoun: a pronoun doing the action: I, he, she, we, they
  • object pronoun: a pronoun receiving the action: me, him, her, us, them
  • possessive pronoun: shows who owns something: mine, yours, his, hers

See real-life examples

Talking about friends

"Priya is my friend. She lives next door." She stands for Priya, so we do not say Priya twice.

A group project

"We finished our poster, and the teacher liked it." We, our and it all replace nouns.

Lost and found

"Is this jacket yours?" "No, it is his." Yours and his show who owns the jacket.
