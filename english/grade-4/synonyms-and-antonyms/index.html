Synonyms and antonyms

Synonyms mean the same. Antonyms mean the opposite.⁠​​‌​‌​​​​‌‌​​​‌‌​​‌​‌​​‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌‌‌​​‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌‌​‌​​​‌‌‌​​‌‌​‌‌​​​​‌​‌‌‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌⁠ Grade 4 English.

Learn what you need to know

A synonym is a word that means the same, or almost the same, as another word. An antonym means the opposite.

😀happy → synonym: glad · antonym: sad
🐘big → synonym: large · antonym: small
⚡fast → synonym: quick · antonym: slow
🥶cold → synonym: chilly · antonym: hot

Writers use synonyms so they do not repeat the same word. Instead of "The big dog ran to the big tree," write "The huge dog ran to the tall tree."

Synonyms are not always exactly the same. Warm and hot are close, but hot is stronger. Pick the one that fits best.

A thesaurus is a book or website that lists synonyms and antonyms.

Memory trick: Synonym = Same. Antonym = Against.

Key words

  • synonym: a word that means the same as another word
  • antonym: a word that means the opposite of another word
  • thesaurus: a book or tool that lists synonyms and antonyms
  • opposite: as different as possible

See real-life examples

Writing a story

Instead of saying "said" every time, writers use synonyms like shouted, whispered and asked.

Signs

Doors often show antonyms: push and pull, in and out, open and closed.

Weather

"Today is hot, tomorrow will be cold." Antonyms help compare two days.
