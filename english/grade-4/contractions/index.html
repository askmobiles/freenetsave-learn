Contractions

Learn what you need to know

A contraction joins two words into one shorter word. An apostrophe ( ' ) goes where the missing letters were.

➕do + not = don't
➕I + am = I'm
➕it + is = it's
➕we + will = we'll
➕they + are = they're
➕she + has = she's

Common endings: n't = not, 're = are, 'll = will, 've = have, 'm = am, 's = is or has, 'd = would or had.

One odd one: will not becomes won't, not "willn't". And can not becomes can't.

Be careful with look-alikes: it's means "it is", but its means "belongs to it". They're means "they are".

Check: split the contraction back into two words. If the sentence still makes sense, you used it correctly.

Key words

  • contraction: two words joined into one shorter word
  • apostrophe: a mark ( ' ) that shows where letters are missing
  • n't: short for not
  • 'll: short for will

See real-life examples

Texting a friend

"I'm late, I'll be there soon!" Contractions make talking and texting quicker.

Signs

"Don't feed the ducks" is shorter than "Do not feed the ducks". Signs need to be short.

Formal writing

In a school report, people often write "do not" instead of "don't" because it sounds more formal.
