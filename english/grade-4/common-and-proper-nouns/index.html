Common and proper nouns

Nouns name people, places and things. Proper nouns get a capital letter.⁠​​‌​‌​​​​‌‌​​​‌‌​​‌​‌​​‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌‌‌​​‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌‌​‌​​​‌‌‌​​‌‌​‌‌​​​​‌​‌‌‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌⁠ Grade 4 English.

Learn what you need to know

A noun names a person, place, thing or idea: girl, city, book, happiness.

👧Common noun: girl → Proper noun: Maya
🏙️Common noun: city → Proper noun: Toronto
🐕Common noun: dog → Proper noun: Rex
📅Common noun: month → Proper noun: October

A common noun is a general word for any person, place or thing. It starts with a small letter unless it begins a sentence.

A proper noun is the name of one special person, place or thing. It always starts with a capital letter: names of people and pets, cities, countries, streets, schools, days, months and holidays.

Seasons (spring, summer, fall, winter) are common nouns, so they do not get a capital letter.

Test: ask "Is this the name of one special one?" If yes, it is a proper noun and needs a capital.

Key words

  • noun: a word that names a person, place, thing or idea
  • common noun: a general name, like river or teacher
  • proper noun: the name of one special person, place or thing
  • capital letter: a big letter, like A, B or C

See real-life examples

Your address

An address is full of proper nouns: the street name, the city and the province all start with capitals.

A calendar

Days, months and holidays are proper nouns: Monday, July, Canada Day.

A map

Maps show proper nouns like Lake Ontario and the Rocky Mountains, which name one special lake and one special mountain range.
