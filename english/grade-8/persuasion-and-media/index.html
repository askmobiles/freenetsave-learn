Persuasion and media

Learn what you need to know

Every media text is made by someone, for an audience, with a purpose: to inform, entertain or persuade (often to sell).

⭐Celebrity endorsement
🐑Bandwagon: "Everyone has one!"
😨Fear: what will happen if you don't buy
💎Snob appeal: be exclusive
🏡Plain folks: ordinary people like you
🔬Scientific claims: "clinically proven"
⏳Urgency: "Today only!"
🎵Emotional appeal: music, cute animals, happy families

Persuasive writing uses ethos (trust), pathos (emotion) and logos (logic and facts).

Online, watch for influencer posts (paid posts must say #ad), targeted ads based on what you search, and edited or AI-made pictures.

Ask: Who made this? Who is it for? What do they want me to do? What is left out?

Key words

  • target audience: the group a message is designed for
  • persuasive technique: a method used to convince an audience
  • ethos, pathos, logos: appeals to trust, emotion and logic
  • influencer: a person paid or sponsored to promote products online
