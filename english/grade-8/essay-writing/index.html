Learn what you need to know

An essay explains or argues a main idea called the thesis. Most Grade 8 essays have five paragraphs.

1️⃣Introduction: a hook to grab attention, some background, and your thesis at the end
2️⃣Body paragraph 1: first reason + evidence + explanation
3️⃣Body paragraph 2: second reason + evidence + explanation
4️⃣Body paragraph 3: third reason + evidence + explanation
5️⃣Conclusion: restate the thesis in new words, sum up, and end with a final thought

A strong thesis is one sentence that states your position and often lists your three reasons: "Schools should have longer lunch breaks because students need time to eat, rest and be active."

Use transitions to connect ideas: first, in addition, furthermore, however, for example, as a result, finally, in conclusion.

Plan before you write: thesis → three reasons → one piece of evidence for each. A 5-minute outline saves time.

Key words

  • thesis: the main idea or argument of an essay, in one sentence
  • hook: an opening that grabs the reader's attention
  • transition: a word or phrase that connects ideas
  • outline: a quick plan of your ideas before writing
