Agreement and pronouns

Make verbs agree with their subjects and pronouns agree with the nouns they replace.⁠​​‌​‌​​​​‌‌​​​‌‌​​‌​‌​​‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌‌‌​​‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌‌​‌​​​‌‌‌​​‌‌​‌‌​​​​‌​‌‌‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌⁠ Grade 8 English.

Learn what you need to know

Subject–verb agreement: a singular subject takes a singular verb; a plural subject takes a plural verb. "The dog barks." "The dogs bark."

🔍Skip the words in between: "The bag of chips is empty." (bag is)
➕Two subjects joined by "and" are plural: "Sam and Ali are here."
🔀With "or/nor", match the closer subject: "Neither the boys nor the coach was late."
1️⃣Each, everyone, nobody, somebody are singular: "Everyone is ready."

Pronoun agreement: a pronoun must match its antecedent (the noun it replaces) in number. "The students packed their bags." "Each student packed their bag" is accepted today because singular "they" is standard when gender is unknown or nonbinary.

Pronoun case: use subject pronouns (I, he, she, we, they) as subjects and object pronouns (me, him, her, us, them) as objects. "Maya and I went." "The coach chose Maya and me."

Trick: remove the other person. "The coach chose (Maya and) me." You would never say "chose I".

Key words

  • subject: who or what the sentence is about
  • antecedent: the noun a pronoun refers to
  • subject pronoun: I, he, she, we, they
  • object pronoun: me, him, her, us, them

See real-life examples

Group chats

"Everyone is coming" sounds right because everyone is singular.

Sports

"The team is winning" treats the team as one unit.

Thank-you notes

"Thank you for inviting my mom and me" is correct.
