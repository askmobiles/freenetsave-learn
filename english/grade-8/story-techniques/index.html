Story techniques

How authors build suspense and meaning: plot structure, foreshadowing, flashback, point of view and conflict.⁠​​‌​‌​​​​‌‌​​​‌‌​​‌​‌​​‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌‌‌​​‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌‌​‌​​​‌‌‌​​‌‌​‌‌​​​​‌​‌‌‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌⁠ Grade 8 English.

Learn what you need to know

Most stories follow a plot arc: exposition (characters and setting), rising action (conflict grows), climax (turning point), falling action, and resolution.

🔮Foreshadowing: hints about what will happen later
⏪Flashback: a scene from the past interrupts the present
🪝Cliffhanger: an ending that leaves the reader in suspense
🎭Dramatic irony: the reader knows something a character does not
⚔️Character vs character
🧠Character vs self (inner struggle)
🌪️Character vs nature
🏛️Character vs society
🤖Character vs technology

Point of view: first person ("I") gives a close, limited view; third person limited follows one character; third person omniscient knows everyone's thoughts.

Show, don't tell: instead of "She was nervous", write "Her hands shook as she reached for the microphone."

Key words

  • foreshadowing: hints about what will happen later in a story
  • flashback: a scene that interrupts the story to show the past
  • climax: the turning point, the moment of greatest tension
  • conflict: the problem or struggle that drives the plot
