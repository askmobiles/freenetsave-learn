Using evidence

Learn what you need to know

Evidence is proof that supports your point. Good evidence is relevant, specific and from a reliable source.

📊Facts and statistics
💬Quotations from a text or an expert
🧪Examples and research results
🧑Personal experience (weaker in formal essays)

Use the sandwich: introduce the evidence, give it, then explain it. Example: "According to Statistics Canada, most Canadians live in cities. This shows that city planning affects most people's daily lives."

Signal phrases: According to…, The author writes…, For example…, This shows that…, This proves…, This means…

Never drop evidence alone. Always explain how it proves your point, and always say where it came from.

Key words

  • evidence: facts, examples or quotations that support a point
  • relevant: directly connected to the point
  • signal phrase: words that introduce evidence, like "According to"
  • analysis: explaining how evidence proves a point
