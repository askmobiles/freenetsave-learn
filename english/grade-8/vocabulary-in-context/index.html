Learn what you need to know

When you meet a new word, look around it. Context clues are hints in the sentence or nearby sentences.

📖Definition: "Herbivores, animals that eat only plants, include deer and rabbits."
🔄Synonym: "The room was frigid, so cold we could see our breath."
↔️Antonym/contrast: "Unlike his gregarious sister, Leo is shy."
🧺Example: "Crustaceans, such as crabs, lobsters and shrimp, have shells."
🧠General sense: "After running the marathon, she was exhausted and fell asleep immediately."

Use word parts: prefixes (un-, re-, pre-, mis-), roots (bio = life, graph = write, port = carry) and suffixes (-less, -ful, -able).

Check your guess by replacing the word with your meaning. Does the sentence still make sense? Then confirm with a dictionary.

Signal words: "such as" = example; "unlike/but/however" = contrast; "or/that is" = definition.

Key words

  • context clue: a hint near an unknown word that helps explain it
  • synonym: a word with a similar meaning
  • antonym: a word with the opposite meaning
  • root: the main part of a word that carries its meaning
