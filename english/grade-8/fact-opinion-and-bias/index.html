Fact, opinion and bias

Learn what you need to know

✅Fact: can be proven true or false — "Canada has ten provinces."
💭Opinion: a belief or feeling — "Canada has the best winters."
⚠️Informed opinion: an opinion supported by evidence — stronger, but still an opinion

Opinion signal words: best, worst, should, I think, beautiful, terrible, always, never.

Bias means favouring one side. Watch for loaded words (positive or negative words that push feelings), missing viewpoints, and who paid for or created the message.

🔍Who made this, and why?
🧭What evidence is given?
🙈What is left out?
🔁Do other reliable sources agree?

Lateral reading: before trusting a website or post, open new tabs and see what trusted sources say about it.

Key words

  • fact: a statement that can be proven true or false
  • opinion: a statement of belief or feeling
  • bias: favouring one side unfairly
  • loaded words: words chosen to create strong positive or negative feelings
