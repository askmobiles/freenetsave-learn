Learn what you need to know

When there is more than one, we often add -s.

🐱one cat → two cats
🍎one apple → three apples
🐶one dog → four dogs
⭐one star → many stars

Look at the number. More than one? Add -s!

Key words

  • one: just 1
  • more than one: 2, 3, 4 or more
  • -s: a letter we add for more than one
