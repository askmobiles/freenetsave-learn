Word families

Word families have the same ending: cat, hat, bat, sat all belong to the "-at" family.⁠​​‌​‌​​​​‌‌​​​‌‌​​‌​‌​​‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌‌‌​​‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌‌​‌​​​‌‌‌​​‌‌​‌‌​​​​‌​‌‌‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌⁠ Grade 1 English.

Learn what you need to know

A word family is a group of words that end the same way. Change the first letter to make a new word!

🐱-at: cat, hat, bat, mat, sat
🐷-ig: pig, big, dig, wig
☀️-un: sun, run, fun, bun
🔥-ot: hot, pot, dot, not
🛏️-ed: bed, red, fed, Ted

If you can read cat, you can read hat, bat and mat too!

Key words

  • word family: words that end with the same letters
  • ending: the last part of a word
  • first letter: the letter at the start of a word
