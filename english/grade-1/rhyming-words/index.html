Rhyming words

Learn what you need to know

Words rhyme when their ending sounds are the same.

🐱cat — hat — bat — mat
🐶dog — log — frog — fog
☀️sun — run — fun — bun
🐝bee — tree — see — three

Say the words out loud. Do they end the same way? They rhyme!

Key words

  • rhyme: words that end with the same sound
  • sound: what you hear
  • ending: the last part of a word
