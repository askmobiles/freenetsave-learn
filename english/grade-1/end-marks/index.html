Learn what you need to know

.Period: a telling sentence. "I have a cat."
?Question mark: an asking sentence. "Do you have a cat?"
!Exclamation mark: a big feeling. "I love my cat!"

Every sentence starts with a capital and ends with a mark.

Key words

  • period: a dot . at the end of a telling sentence
  • question mark: the mark ? at the end of a question
  • exclamation mark: the mark ! for a big feeling
