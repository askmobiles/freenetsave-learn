Learn what you need to know

A sentence is a group of words that tells a whole idea.

✅Sentence: "The cat sleeps." — it tells who and what happens
❌Not a sentence: "The cat." — what about the cat?
❌Not a sentence: "sleeps on the bed" — who sleeps?

A sentence tells who or what and what happens. It starts with a capital and ends with . ? or !

Ask: Does it make sense all by itself? Then it is a sentence!

Key words

  • sentence: words that tell a whole idea
  • idea: a thought
  • whole: all of it, not just part
