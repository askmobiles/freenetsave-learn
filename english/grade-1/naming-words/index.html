Naming words

Learn what you need to know

A naming word names a person, an animal, a place or a thing.

👧girl
🐶dog
🏠house
⚽ball
🌳tree
🚗car

Naming words are also called nouns.

Key words

  • naming word: a word that names a person, animal, place or thing
  • noun: another name for a naming word
  • thing: something you can see or touch
