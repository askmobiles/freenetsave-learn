Learn what you need to know

The alphabet goes: a b c d e f g h i j k l m n o p q r s t u v w x y z.

To put words in ABC order, look at the first letter of each word.

🍎apple (a)
🐻bear (b)
🐱cat (c)
🐶dog (d)

Sing the ABC song to help you!

Key words

  • alphabet: all the letters from a to z
  • ABC order: putting words in the order of the alphabet
  • first letter: the letter at the start of a word
