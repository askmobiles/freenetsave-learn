Learn what you need to know

Capital letters are big letters: A B C D E F G...

1️⃣Start every sentence with a capital letter: The dog runs.
👤Names start with a capital letter: Sam, Maya, Buddy
🙋The word I is always a capital letter.

Small letters: a b c. Capital letters: A B C.

Key words

  • capital letter: a big letter, like A, B, C
  • small letter: a little letter, like a, b, c
  • sentence: words that tell a whole idea
