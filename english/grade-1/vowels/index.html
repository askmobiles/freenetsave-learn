Learn what you need to know

There are five vowels: a, e, i, o, u. All the other letters are consonants.

🍎a — apple, cat
🥚e — egg, bed
🍦i — igloo, pig
🐙o — octopus, dog
☂️u — umbrella, bug

Sometimes y acts like a vowel too: my, happy.

Key words

  • vowel: the letters a, e, i, o, u
  • consonant: all the other letters, like b, c, d
  • sound: what you hear
