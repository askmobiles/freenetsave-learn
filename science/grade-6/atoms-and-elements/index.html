Learn what you need to know

All matter is made of tiny particles called atoms. Atoms are so small that a million of them side by side would be thinner than a hair.

➕Protons: positive charge, in the nucleus
⚪Neutrons: no charge, in the nucleus
➖Electrons: negative charge, zip around the nucleus

An element is a pure substance made of only one kind of atom. Gold, oxygen, iron and carbon are elements. There are 118 known elements, listed in the periodic table.

Each element has a short symbol: O for oxygen, H for hydrogen, C for carbon, Fe for iron, Au for gold.

When atoms of different elements join, they make compounds. Water (H₂O) is two hydrogen atoms joined to one oxygen atom.

Remember: atoms make elements; different elements join to make compounds.

Key words

  • atom: the tiny particle that makes up all matter
  • element: a pure substance made of one kind of atom
  • compound: two or more elements joined together
  • periodic table: a chart of all the elements

See real-life examples

Water

Water is a compound: H₂O means two hydrogen atoms and one oxygen atom.

Pencil "lead"

The "lead" in a pencil is really graphite, a form of the element carbon. Diamonds are carbon too!

Balloons

Party balloons that float are filled with helium, a very light element.
