Properties of air

Air takes up space, has mass, exerts pressure and expands when heated. Grade 6 science.

📘 Ontario Grade 6 · Flight

Learn what you need to know

Air is a mixture of gases: about 78% nitrogen, 21% oxygen, and small amounts of argon, carbon dioxide and water vapour.

📦Air takes up space: an "empty" bottle is full of air
⚖️Air has mass: a full ball weighs more than a flat one
🎈Air can be compressed (squeezed): like in a bike tire
🔥Air expands when heated and contracts when cooled
⬇️Air exerts pressure in all directions

Warm air is less dense than cool air, so it rises. That is how hot-air balloons fly.

Moving air has lower pressure than still air. This idea, called Bernoulli's principle, helps explain lift on wings.

Remember: air takes up space, has mass, can be squeezed, and warm air rises.

Key words

  • air pressure: the push of air on everything around it
  • compress: to squeeze into a smaller space
  • expand: to take up more space
  • Bernoulli's principle: faster moving air has lower pressure

See real-life examples

Hot-air balloons

A burner heats the air inside the balloon. Warm air is less dense, so the balloon rises.

Bike tires

A pump squeezes (compresses) lots of air into a tire so it stays firm.

Popping ears

Driving up a mountain, air pressure drops, and your ears "pop" as pressure balances.
