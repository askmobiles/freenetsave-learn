Learn what you need to know

The cell theory has three main ideas:

1️⃣All living things are made of one or more cells
2️⃣The cell is the basic unit of life
3️⃣All cells come from other cells

In big organisms like you, cells are specialized: their shape fits their job. Red blood cells are disc-shaped to carry oxygen. Nerve cells are long to carry messages. Muscle cells can contract. Root hair cells are long and thin to absorb water.

Cells grow and make copies of themselves by cell division. In mitosis, one cell divides into two identical cells. This is how you grow and how cuts heal. Cancer is when cells divide out of control.

Cells are organized: cells → tissues → organs → organ systems → organism.

Remember: all cells come from cells, and specialized cells have shapes that fit their jobs.

Key words

  • cell theory: the idea that all living things are made of cells that come from other cells
  • specialized cell: a cell with a special shape and job
  • mitosis: cell division that makes two identical cells
  • tissue: a group of similar cells doing the same job

See real-life examples

Red blood cells

Red blood cells have no nucleus and are shaped like squished doughnuts, giving them more room to carry oxygen.

Growing up

You grew from one single cell into trillions of cells, all by cell division.

Cancer research

Canadian scientists discovered stem cells in 1961. Today researchers study how to stop cells that divide out of control.
