Learn what you need to know

Biodiversity exists at three levels: genetic diversity (variety within a species), species diversity (number of species), and ecosystem diversity (variety of habitats).

Scientists measure biodiversity with tools like quadrats (squares placed on the ground to count organisms) and transects.

The biggest threats are remembered as HIPPO:

🏗️H: Habitat loss
🐚I: Invasive species
🏭P: Pollution
👥P: Population growth (human)
🎣O: Overharvesting

Ecosystems provide ecosystem services: clean air and water, pollination, flood control, soil, food and medicine.

Sustainability means meeting today's needs without harming future generations. Stewardship includes protected areas, species-at-risk laws (Canada's Species at Risk Act), sustainable forestry and fishing, and respecting Indigenous knowledge of the land.

Remember: HIPPO threatens biodiversity; sustainability protects it for the future.

Key words

  • genetic diversity: variety of genes within one species
  • quadrat: a square frame used to sample organisms
  • ecosystem services: benefits people get from healthy ecosystems
  • stewardship: responsibly caring for the environment
