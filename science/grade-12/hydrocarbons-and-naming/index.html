Hydrocarbons and naming

Alkanes, alkenes, alkynes, cyclic and aromatic hydrocarbons, isomers and IUPAC naming.⁠​​‌​‌​​​​‌‌​​​‌‌​​‌​‌​​‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌‌‌​​‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌​‌‌‌​‌​​​‌‌‌​​‌‌​‌‌​​​​‌​‌‌‌​‌‌​​‌‌​​‌​‌⁠ Grade 12 science.

Learn what you need to know

Organic compounds contain carbon (usually with hydrogen). Carbon makes 4 bonds, so it forms chains, branches and rings. Hydrocarbons contain only C and H.

➖Alkanes: single bonds only, CₙH₂ₙ₊₂, names end in -ane (propane)
🟰Alkenes: at least one C=C, CₙH₂ₙ, end in -ene (but-2-ene)
🔱Alkynes: at least one C≡C, CₙH₂ₙ₋₂, end in -yne (ethyne)
💍Cyclic (cyclohexane) and aromatic (benzene, a ring of 6 C with delocalized electrons)

Prefixes for chain length: meth (1), eth (2), prop (3), but (4), pent (5), hex (6), hept (7), oct (8), non (9), dec (10). Branches are alkyl groups: methyl (–CH₃), ethyl (–C₂H₅).

IUPAC steps: 1) find the longest chain that contains any double or triple bond; 2) number it so the multiple bond (then branches) get the lowest numbers; 3) name branches with their position, in alphabetical order, using di-, tri- for repeats; 4) e.g. 2,3-dimethylpentane, 3-methylbut-1-ene.

Isomers have the same formula but a different structure. Structural isomers: butane and 2-methylpropane (both C₄H₁₀). Geometric (cis/trans) isomers happen around a C=C because it cannot rotate: cis = same side, trans = opposite sides.

Remember: longest chain, lowest numbers, alphabetical branches. -ane single, -ene double, -yne triple.

Key words

  • saturated: a hydrocarbon with only single C–C bonds
  • alkyl group: a branch made by removing one H from an alkane, like methyl
  • isomer: same molecular formula, different structure
  • aromatic: containing a benzene-type ring

See real-life examples

Barbecue fuel

Propane (C₃H₈) and butane (C₄H₁₀) are alkanes used in barbecues and lighters.

Welding torches

Ethyne (acetylene), an alkyne, burns very hot for cutting and welding metal.

Ripening fruit

Ethene (ethylene) is a plant hormone that makes bananas and tomatoes ripen.
