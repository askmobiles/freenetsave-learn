Kinds of force

Gravity, friction, magnetism and more. Grade 3 science.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn what you need to know

A force is a push or a pull. Some forces need touch. Others work from a distance.

🌍Gravity pulls things down toward Earth
🛑Friction slows things that rub together
🧲Magnetic force pulls iron and steel
⚡Static electricity makes hair stick to a balloon
💪Muscle force: pushes and pulls you make
🌊Water and wind push things

Contact forces need touching, like friction and kicking a ball. Non-contact forces work without touching, like gravity, magnets and static electricity.

Forces can make things start moving, stop, speed up, slow down, change direction or change shape (like squashing clay).

Remember: gravity pulls down, friction slows down, magnets pull iron and steel.

Key words

  • gravity: the force that pulls things toward Earth
  • friction: a force that slows things when they rub
  • static electricity: a charge that makes things stick or spark
  • contact force: a force that needs touching

See real-life examples

Balloon hair

Rub a balloon on your hair. Your hair rises toward the balloon. That is static electricity, a force that works without touching.

Bike brakes

Bike brakes squeeze the wheel. Friction slows it down so you can stop safely.

Dropped apple

When you drop an apple, gravity pulls it to the floor every time.
