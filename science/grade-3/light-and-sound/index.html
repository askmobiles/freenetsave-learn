Light and sound

How we see and how we hear. Grade 3 science.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn what you need to know

Light lets us see. Light comes from light sources like the Sun, fire, lamps and screens. Light travels in straight lines.

We see an object when light bounces off it and goes into our eyes. Light bounces well off shiny things like mirrors. This bouncing is called reflection.

🪟Transparent: lets light through (clear glass)
🧻Translucent: lets some light through (wax paper)
🧱Opaque: blocks light and makes shadows (wood, you)

Sound is made when something vibrates (shakes back and forth quickly). A guitar string, a drum and your voice box all vibrate.

Fast vibrations make high sounds (high pitch). Slow vibrations make low sounds. Big vibrations make loud sounds.

Remember: light travels in straight lines and reflects; sound is made by vibrations.

Key words

  • light source: something that gives off its own light
  • reflect: bounce off
  • opaque: blocks all light
  • vibrate: shake back and forth quickly
  • pitch: how high or low a sound is

See real-life examples

Shadow puppets

Your hand is opaque, so it blocks the light from a flashlight and makes a shadow on the wall.

Feel your voice

Put your fingers on your throat and hum. You can feel your voice box vibrating.

Thunder and lightning

You see lightning before you hear thunder because light travels much faster than sound.
