Organs of the body

The brain, heart, lungs, stomach and more. Grade 3 science.

⭐ Extra topic · Ontario teaches this in Grade 5 (Human Health and Body Systems)

Learn what you need to know

An organ is a body part that does a special job. Organs work together to keep you alive.

🧠Brain: thinks and controls your body
❤️Heart: pumps blood all around your body
🫁Lungs: breathe in air, take in oxygen
🍽️Stomach: breaks down food
🧪Kidneys: clean your blood and make urine
🧍Skin: the largest organ, protects your body

Your heart is about the size of your fist. It beats about 100,000 times every day!

Your lungs fill with air when you breathe in. The oxygen goes into your blood, and your heart pumps it to every part of your body.

Your brain sends messages along your nerves to tell your muscles to move.

Remember: brain thinks, heart pumps, lungs breathe, stomach digests.

Key words

  • organ: a body part with a special job
  • pulse: the beat of your heart you can feel in your wrist
  • oxygen: the gas from air your body needs
  • nerves: thin paths that carry messages to and from the brain

See real-life examples

Feel your pulse

Put two fingers on the inside of your wrist. Each little bump is your heartbeat pushing blood.

After running

After a race you breathe fast and your heart beats fast. Your muscles need more oxygen.

Ouch!

When you touch something hot, nerves send a message to your brain so fast that you pull your hand away right away.
