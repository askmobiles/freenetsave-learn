Learn what you need to know

Air is all around us. We cannot see it, but we can feel it when the wind blows. All living things need air.

Water covers most of the Earth. It is in oceans, lakes, rivers, rain and snow. All living things need water.

💨Wind is moving air
🌬️We breathe air
🌊Oceans hold salt water
🏞️Lakes and rivers hold fresh water
🌧️Rain and snow bring water
🚰We drink fresh water

Water moves around in the water cycle. The Sun warms water so it rises into the air. It makes clouds, then falls back as rain or snow.

We must keep air and water clean. Do not litter. Walk or bike instead of driving when you can. Turn off the tap while you brush your teeth.

Remember: all living things need clean air and water.

Key words

  • air: the gas all around us that we breathe
  • fresh water: water that is not salty, in lakes and rivers
  • pollution: dirt and harmful things in air, water or land
  • water cycle: water going up into clouds and coming down as rain

See real-life examples

Feeling air

Wave your hand in front of your face. You can feel the air moving even though you cannot see it.

Saving water

Turning off the tap while brushing your teeth can save many litres of water every day.

Great Lakes

The Great Lakes hold a huge amount of fresh water. Many Canadians drink water that comes from them.
