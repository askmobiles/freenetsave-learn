Learn what you need to know

A life cycle is the way a living thing is born, grows, has young, and the cycle starts again.

A butterfly has four stages:

🥚1. egg
🐛2. caterpillar (larva)
🛌3. chrysalis (pupa)
🦋4. butterfly (adult)

A frog also changes a lot:

🥚1. eggs in water
〰️2. tadpole with a tail
🐸3. froglet with legs
🐸4. adult frog

Some animals, like dogs and people, look a lot like their parents from the start. They just get bigger.

Remember: born → grow → have young → the cycle starts again.

Key words

  • life cycle: the stages a living thing goes through
  • larva: the young stage of an insect, like a caterpillar
  • pupa: the resting stage when an insect changes
  • adult: fully grown

See real-life examples

Monarch butterflies

A monarch caterpillar eats milkweed leaves, then hangs upside down and makes a green chrysalis. About two weeks later, a butterfly comes out.

A chicken

A hen lays an egg. A chick hatches, grows feathers and becomes a hen or rooster.

You

You started as a baby, now you are a child, and one day you will be an adult.
