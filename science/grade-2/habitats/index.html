Learn what you need to know

A habitat is the home where an animal or plant lives. It gives them food, water, shelter and space.

🌲Forest: deer, owls, squirrels
🌊Ocean: whales, fish, crabs
🏜️Desert: camels, lizards, cactus
🧊Arctic: polar bears, seals
🏞️Pond: frogs, ducks, fish
🌾Grassland: bison, gophers

Animals have body parts that help them live in their habitat. A polar bear has thick fur for the cold. A camel can go days without water in the hot desert. A duck has webbed feet for swimming.

When a habitat is harmed, by pollution or cutting down trees, the animals lose their homes.

Remember: a habitat gives food, water, shelter and space.

Key words

  • habitat: the natural home of a plant or animal
  • Arctic: the very cold land near the North Pole
  • desert: a very dry place with little rain

See real-life examples

A beaver's pond

Beavers build dams across streams. This makes a pond where they build their lodge and stay safe.

Polar bears

Polar bears live on the Arctic sea ice in northern Canada. They hunt seals there.

Your backyard

Even a backyard is a habitat. Worms, ants, robins and squirrels may live there.
