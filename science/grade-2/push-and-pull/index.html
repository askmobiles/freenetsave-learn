Push and pull

Forces make things start, stop and change direction. Grade 2 science.

📘 Ontario Grade 2 · Simple Machines and Movement

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn what you need to know

A force is a push or a pull. Forces make things move, stop, speed up, slow down or change direction.

🛒Push a shopping cart
🚪Pull a door open
⚽Kick a ball (push)
🪁Pull a kite string
🛷Pull a sled
🔘Push a button

A bigger push or pull makes things move faster or farther. Heavy things need a bigger force to move.

Gravity is a pull. It pulls everything down toward the Earth. That is why a ball comes back down when you throw it up.

Remember: every force is a push or a pull.

Key words

  • force: a push or a pull
  • push: moving something away from you
  • pull: moving something toward you
  • gravity: the pull that makes things fall down

See real-life examples

Soccer

When you kick the ball softly it rolls a little. When you kick it hard it rolls far. A bigger force makes it go farther.

Tug of war

Two teams pull a rope. The team that pulls harder wins.

Swings

A friend pushes you on the swing. Gravity pulls you back down.
