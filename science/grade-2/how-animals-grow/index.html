How animals grow

Animals grow bigger and change as they get older. Grade 2 science.

📘 Ontario Grade 2 · Growth and Changes in Animals

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn what you need to know

All animals grow. Young animals need food, water, shelter and, often, care from their parents.

Some animals grow by getting bigger. A kitten grows into a cat. A calf grows into a cow.

Some animals must change their outside to grow. A snake sheds its old skin. A crab sheds its hard shell and grows a bigger one. A caterpillar changes completely into a butterfly.

🐍A snake sheds its skin
🦀A crab grows a new shell
🦌A deer grows new antlers each year
🐣A chick grows feathers

People grow too. You lose your baby teeth and grow adult teeth. You grow taller every year.

Remember: animals grow bigger, and some change their skin, shell or whole body.

Key words

  • grow: get bigger and change
  • shed: let an old skin or shell fall off
  • young: a baby animal

See real-life examples

Snake skin

A snake's skin does not stretch, so it rubs its old skin off on a rock and has a new, bigger skin under it.

Lobster shells

A lobster sheds its shell many times as it grows. It hides until its new shell gets hard.

Your teeth

Around age 6, children start losing baby teeth. Adult teeth grow in their place.
