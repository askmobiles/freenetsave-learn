The seasons

How plants, animals and people change through the year. Grade 2 science.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn what you need to know

In Canada, the year has four seasons: spring, summer, fall (autumn) and winter. Each one has its own weather and length of day.

🌷Spring: warmer, rainy, plants sprout, birds return
🌞Summer: hot, long days, plants grow most
🍂Fall: cooler, leaves change colour and drop, animals store food
⛄Winter: cold, short days, snow, some animals sleep

Animals change with the seasons. Squirrels store nuts in fall. Bears hibernate (sleep deeply) in winter. Geese migrate south in fall and come back in spring.

People change too. We wear different clothes and do different activities, like swimming in summer and skating in winter.

Remember: spring, summer, fall, winter. Animals hibernate, migrate or store food.

Key words

  • hibernate: sleep deeply through the winter
  • migrate: travel far away for a season
  • autumn: another name for fall

See real-life examples

Canada geese

In fall, Canada geese fly south in a V shape. In spring they return north to have their babies.

Maple syrup

In early spring, maple sap starts to flow. People collect it and boil it into maple syrup.

Short winter days

In December it gets dark around 5 p.m. In June it is still light at 9 p.m.
