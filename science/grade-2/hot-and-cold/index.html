Learn what you need to know

Temperature tells us how hot or cold something is. We measure it with a thermometer.

Heat makes things warm. The Sun, a stove, a fire and your own body all give heat.

☀️The Sun gives heat
🔥Fire gives heat
🧤Mittens keep heat in
🧊Ice keeps food cold
🌡️A thermometer measures temperature
🧥A coat traps your body heat

Heat changes things. It melts ice and chocolate. It cooks food. Cold freezes water into ice.

Some materials keep heat in. A wool hat keeps your head warm. A cooler bag keeps drinks cold.

Remember: a thermometer measures temperature; heat melts, cold freezes.

Key words

  • temperature: how hot or cold something is
  • thermometer: a tool that measures temperature
  • heat: the energy that makes things warm

See real-life examples

Reading a thermometer

When it gets warmer, the red line in a thermometer goes up. When it gets colder, it goes down.

Hot chocolate

A cup of hot chocolate slowly cools down. Heat moves from the hot drink into the cooler air.

Ice packs

An ice pack in your lunch bag keeps your yogurt cold until lunch.
