What plants need

Light, water, air, soil and space. Grade 2 science.

⭐ Extra topic · Ontario teaches this in Grade 3 (Growth and Changes in Plants)

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn what you need to know

Plants are living things. To grow, they need:

☀️light
💧water
🌬️air
🟫soil with nutrients
📏space
🌡️the right temperature

Plants make their own food in their leaves using sunlight, air and water. This is why they need light.

Soil gives plants water and nutrients, which are like vitamins for plants. Roots hold the plant in the soil.

If a plant gets too little water, it wilts (droops). If it gets no light, it turns pale and weak.

Remember: light, water, air, soil and space help plants grow.

Key words

  • nutrients: things in soil that help plants grow strong
  • wilt: droop because of not enough water
  • sprout: begin to grow from a seed

See real-life examples

Bean experiment

Plant two bean seeds. Water one and not the other. Only the watered seed sprouts and grows.

Toward the light

A plant on a windowsill leans toward the window to get more light.

Crowded garden

Carrots planted too close together stay small. Thinning them out gives each one space to grow.
