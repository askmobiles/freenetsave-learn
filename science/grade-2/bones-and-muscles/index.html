Bones and muscles

Your skeleton holds you up and your muscles help you move. Grade 2 science.

⭐ Extra topic · Ontario teaches this in Grade 5 (Human Health and Body Systems)

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn what you need to know

Inside your body are bones. All your bones together make your skeleton. An adult has 206 bones.

💀The skull protects your brain
🫁The ribs protect your heart and lungs
🦴The backbone (spine) helps you stand up
🦵Leg bones hold up your body

Your skeleton holds you up, gives your body its shape and protects soft parts inside.

Muscles are attached to your bones. When a muscle pulls, the bone moves. Your heart is a muscle too. It pumps all day and night.

Bones meet at joints, like your knees, elbows and shoulders. Joints let you bend.

Remember: bones hold you up, muscles move you, joints let you bend.

Key words

  • skeleton: all the bones in your body
  • muscle: a body part that pulls to make you move
  • joint: where two bones meet, like a knee
  • skull: the bones that protect your brain

See real-life examples

Bend your arm

Make a fist and bend your elbow. Feel the muscle on top of your arm get hard. It is pulling your arm up.

Helmets

Your skull protects your brain, but a helmet gives extra protection when you bike or skate.

Strong bones

Milk, yogurt, cheese and leafy greens help build strong bones. So do running and jumping.
