Learn what you need to know

Things can move in many different ways.

⚽roll
🛝slide
🌀spin
🏀bounce
🎠go round and round
🕰️swing back and forth

Round things roll. Flat things slide. A top spins. A swing moves back and forth. A ball bounces up and down.

Things move faster on smooth surfaces and slower on rough ones. A toy car rolls far on a smooth floor but stops quickly on a carpet. This slowing is called friction.

Remember: round things roll, flat things slide; rough surfaces slow things down.

Key words

  • roll: turn over and over on a round shape
  • slide: move smoothly along a surface
  • friction: the rubbing that slows things down

See real-life examples

Ice skating

Skates slide easily on smooth ice. On grass they would hardly move. Ice has very little friction.

Wheels

Wheels roll, which makes it much easier to move heavy things. That is why carts, bikes and cars have wheels.

Playground

On a playground you swing, slide, spin on a roundabout and bounce on a trampoline.
