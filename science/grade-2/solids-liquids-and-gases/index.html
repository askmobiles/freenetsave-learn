Solids, liquids and gases

The three states of matter. Grade 2 science.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn what you need to know

Everything around you is matter. Matter comes in three main forms, called states.

🧊Solid: keeps its own shape (ice, rock, book)
💧Liquid: flows and takes the shape of its container (water, milk, juice)
💨Gas: spreads out to fill any space (air, steam)

Water can be all three! Ice is solid water. Water from the tap is liquid. Steam from a kettle is a gas.

Remember: solids keep their shape, liquids flow, gases spread out.

Key words

  • matter: anything that takes up space
  • solid: keeps its shape
  • liquid: flows and can be poured
  • gas: spreads out to fill a space

See real-life examples

Juice in a cup

Pour juice from a box into a cup. The juice takes the shape of the cup. Liquids take the shape of their container.

A balloon

Blow up a balloon. The air inside spreads out to fill the whole balloon. Air is a gas.

Melting ice cream

Ice cream is solid in the freezer. On a hot day it melts into a liquid.
