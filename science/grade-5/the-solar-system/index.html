Learn what you need to know

Our solar system is the Sun and everything that orbits it: eight planets, their moons, dwarf planets like Pluto, asteroids and comets.

The Sun is a star. It holds 99.8% of all the mass in the solar system, and its gravity keeps everything in orbit.

☿Mercury: closest, smallest
♀Venus: hottest, thick clouds
🌍Earth: water and life
🔴Mars: red, rocky
🟠Jupiter: biggest, gas giant
🪐Saturn: famous rings
🔵Uranus: tipped on its side
🔷Neptune: farthest, windy

The four inner planets (Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars) are small and rocky. The four outer planets (Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune) are huge gas and ice giants. Between Mars and Jupiter is the asteroid belt.

A memory trick: My Very Excellent Mother Just Served Us Nachos.

Remember: 4 rocky inner planets, the asteroid belt, then 4 giant outer planets.

Key words

  • planet: a large round object that orbits a star
  • orbit: the path around another object
  • asteroid: a rocky chunk that orbits the Sun
  • comet: a ball of ice and dust with a glowing tail near the Sun

See real-life examples

Canadarm

Canada built the Canadarm robotic arms used on the Space Shuttle and the International Space Station.

Mars rovers

Robot rovers drive across Mars, taking pictures and testing rocks to learn if water once flowed there.

Shooting stars

A "shooting star" is not a star. It is a tiny bit of space dust burning up in Earth's air.
