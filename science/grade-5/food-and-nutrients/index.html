Food and nutrients

Nutrients, Canada's Food Guide and healthy choices. Grade 5 science.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn what you need to know

Food gives your body nutrients: the things it needs for energy, growth and repair.

🍞Carbohydrates: main energy source (bread, rice, fruit)
🍗Proteins: build and repair muscles (beans, fish, eggs, meat, tofu)
🥑Fats: stored energy and warmth (nuts, oils, avocado)
🍊Vitamins: help your body work (vitamin C in oranges)
🥛Minerals: build bones and blood (calcium, iron)
💧Water: you need it every day

Canada's Food Guide suggests filling half your plate with vegetables and fruits, a quarter with whole grain foods, and a quarter with protein foods, and making water your drink of choice.

Fibre in whole grains, fruits and vegetables helps digestion. Too much sugar, salt and processed food can harm your health over time.

Remember: half veggies and fruits, a quarter whole grains, a quarter protein, and drink water.

Key words

  • nutrient: a part of food your body needs
  • carbohydrate: a nutrient that gives quick energy
  • protein: a nutrient that builds and repairs the body
  • vitamin: a nutrient that helps the body work properly

See real-life examples

A balanced lunch

A whole wheat wrap with chicken, lettuce and peppers, plus an apple and water, covers every part of the plate.

Reading labels

Food labels show sugar, salt and fat. Comparing labels helps you choose the healthier cereal.

Iron and energy

Iron in beans, spinach and meat helps your blood carry oxygen. Too little iron can make you feel tired.
