Learn what you need to know

Your body is organized in levels: cells make tissues, tissues make organs, and organs work together in organ systems.

🦴Skeletal: bones support and protect
💪Muscular: muscles move you
🫁Respiratory: lungs take in oxygen
❤️Circulatory: heart and blood carry oxygen and food
🍽️Digestive: breaks down food
🧠Nervous: brain and nerves send messages

Systems work together. When you run, your nervous system tells your muscles to move, your respiratory system breathes faster to get more oxygen, and your circulatory system pumps it to your muscles.

Keep your systems healthy: eat well, drink water, exercise, sleep, wear a helmet, and avoid smoking.

Remember: cells → tissues → organs → systems, all working as a team.

Key words

  • cell: the smallest living unit of the body
  • tissue: a group of similar cells working together
  • organ system: organs that work together for one job
  • nervous system: the brain, spinal cord and nerves

See real-life examples

Catching a ball

Your eyes see the ball, your brain decides, nerves tell muscles to move, and your bones and joints let your arm reach.

Eating lunch

Your digestive system breaks down your sandwich, and your circulatory system carries the nutrients to every cell.

A fever

When you are sick, your immune system fights germs. A fever is one way your body fights infection.
