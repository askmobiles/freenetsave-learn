Forms of energy

Kinetic, potential, thermal, light, sound, electrical and chemical energy. Grade 5 science.

📘 Ontario Grade 5 · Conservation of Energy and Resources

Learn what you need to know

Energy is the ability to do work or cause change. Energy comes in many forms.

🏃Kinetic: energy of motion
🪨Potential: stored energy (a stretched spring, a rock on a cliff)
🔥Thermal: heat energy
💡Light energy
🔊Sound energy
⚡Electrical energy
🍎Chemical energy: stored in food, fuel and batteries
☢️Nuclear energy: stored in the centre of atoms

Energy is never made or destroyed. It transforms (changes) from one form to another. A flashlight changes chemical energy in batteries into electrical energy, then into light and some heat.

A roller coaster at the top of a hill has lots of potential energy. As it races down, that turns into kinetic energy.

Remember: energy changes form, but it is never used up or created from nothing.

Key words

  • kinetic energy: the energy of moving things
  • potential energy: stored energy waiting to be used
  • transform: change from one form to another
  • chemical energy: energy stored in food, fuel and batteries

See real-life examples

Bouncing ball

A ball held high has potential energy. When you drop it, it turns into kinetic energy. Each bounce loses a little as sound and heat.

Eating breakfast

Food holds chemical energy. Your body changes it into kinetic energy to move and thermal energy to stay warm.

Hydroelectric dams

Water held behind a dam has potential energy. Falling water spins turbines that make electrical energy. Much of Ontario's electricity comes this way.
