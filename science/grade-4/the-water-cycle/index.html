The water cycle

Evaporation, condensation, precipitation and collection. Grade 4 science.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn what you need to know

The water on Earth is used again and again. It moves in a never-ending loop called the water cycle.

☀️1. Evaporation: the Sun heats water and it rises as water vapour
☁️2. Condensation: vapour cools and forms tiny drops in clouds
🌧️3. Precipitation: drops get heavy and fall as rain, snow, sleet or hail
🏞️4. Collection: water gathers in oceans, lakes, rivers and underground

The Sun powers the water cycle with its heat. Plants also add water vapour to the air through their leaves. This is called transpiration.

Remember: evaporation → condensation → precipitation → collection, over and over.

Key words

  • evaporation: liquid water turning into water vapour
  • condensation: water vapour cooling into liquid drops
  • precipitation: water falling from clouds: rain, snow, sleet, hail
  • water vapour: water in the form of a gas

See real-life examples

Cold glass

Water drops form on the outside of a cold glass on a hot day. Water vapour in the air condenses on the cold glass.

Drying laundry

Wet clothes on a line dry because the water evaporates into the air.

Bathroom mirror

After a hot shower, the mirror fogs up. Steam condenses on the cool glass.
