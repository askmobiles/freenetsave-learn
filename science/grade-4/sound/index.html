Learn what you need to know

Sound is a form of energy made when things vibrate. The vibrations travel as sound waves through air, water or solids to your ears.

🎸A guitar string vibrates
🥁A drum skin vibrates
🗣️Your vocal cords vibrate
👂Your eardrum vibrates when sound arrives

Pitch is how high or low a sound is. Fast vibrations make high pitch (a whistle). Slow vibrations make low pitch (a tuba). Short, thin, tight strings vibrate faster.

Volume is how loud or soft a sound is. Bigger vibrations make louder sounds. Volume is measured in decibels (dB). Very loud sounds, over about 85 dB, can damage hearing.

Sound cannot travel through empty space, because there is nothing to vibrate. Sound travels faster through water and solids than through air. An echo is sound bouncing off a hard surface.

Remember: vibrations make sound; fast = high pitch, big = loud.

Key words

  • vibrate: move back and forth quickly
  • pitch: how high or low a sound is
  • volume: how loud or soft a sound is
  • echo: sound bouncing back off a surface

See real-life examples

String telephone

Two cups joined by a tight string let you hear a friend. The string carries the vibrations.

Bats

Bats make high squeaks and listen for the echoes to find insects in the dark. This is called echolocation.

Ear protection

People who use loud machines wear ear protection, because loud noise can damage hearing forever.
