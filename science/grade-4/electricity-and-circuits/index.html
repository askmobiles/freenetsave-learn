Electricity and circuits

How a circuit works, conductors, insulators and safety. Grade 4 science.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn what you need to know

Electricity is energy that flows through wires. It powers lights, computers and fridges.

A circuit is a complete loop that electricity flows around. A simple circuit has a battery (energy source), wires, and a load like a light bulb. A switch opens and closes the loop.

A closed circuit has no gaps, so the bulb lights. An open circuit has a gap, so the electricity stops.

🔩Conductors let electricity flow: copper, iron, aluminum
🧤Insulators block it: plastic, rubber, wood, glass

Safety: never touch outlets with wet hands, never poke things into outlets, and stay far away from power lines.

Remember: a complete loop lights the bulb; metals conduct, plastic and rubber insulate.

Key words

  • circuit: a complete path for electricity
  • conductor: a material that lets electricity flow
  • insulator: a material that blocks electricity
  • switch: opens or closes a circuit

See real-life examples

Light switch

When you flip a light switch on, you close the circuit. Flip it off and the circuit opens.

Plastic covered wires

Wires have copper inside to carry electricity and plastic outside to keep you safe.

Holiday lights

In some old strings of lights, if one bulb breaks, the whole string goes out, because the loop is broken.
