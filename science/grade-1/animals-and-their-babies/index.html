Animals and their babies

Baby animal names and how animals grow. Grade 1 science.

📘 Ontario Grade 1 · Needs and Characteristics of Living Things

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn what you need to know

Animals have babies. Some babies look like their parents. Some look very different and change as they grow.

🐶dog → puppy
🐱cat → kitten
🐄cow → calf
🐑sheep → lamb
🐴horse → foal
🐸frog → tadpole

Some animals are born alive, like puppies and kittens. Others hatch from eggs, like chicks, ducklings and baby turtles.

Many parents feed and protect their babies. Birds bring worms to their chicks. Mammals feed their babies milk.

Remember: some babies are born, some hatch from eggs, and they all grow up.

Key words

  • hatch: break out of an egg
  • mammal: an animal with hair or fur that feeds its babies milk
  • young: baby animals

See real-life examples

Frog changes

A frog lays eggs in water. A tadpole hatches and swims with a tail. It grows legs, loses its tail and becomes a frog.

Kangaroo

A baby kangaroo, called a joey, lives in its mother's pouch.

Ducklings

Ducklings hatch from eggs and follow their mother in a line.
