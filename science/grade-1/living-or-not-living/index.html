Living or not living

How can you tell if something is alive? Grade 1 science.

📘 Ontario Grade 1 · Needs and Characteristics of Living Things

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn what you need to know

Some things are living. People, animals and plants are living things. Some things are not living. Rocks, toys and water are not living.

Living things all do the same jobs. That is how we can tell they are alive.

🍎They need food
💧They need water
🌬️They need air
📏They grow
🐣They make babies
🏃They move or react

A rock does none of these things. It does not eat, grow or have babies. So a rock is not living.

Some things were living once, like a dry leaf or a wooden chair. They are not living now.

Remember: if it eats, drinks, breathes, grows and makes babies, it is living.

Key words

  • living: alive: it grows and needs food, water and air
  • not living: never alive, like a rock or a toy
  • grow: get bigger

See real-life examples

A cat and a toy cat

The real cat eats, drinks and grows. It can have kittens. The toy cat never eats or grows. The real cat is living. The toy cat is not living.

A tree

A tree does not walk, but it is living. It drinks water through its roots, grows taller every year and makes seeds that grow into new trees.

A car

A car moves and needs gas, but it does not grow and cannot have baby cars. A car is not living.
