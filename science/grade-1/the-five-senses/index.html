The five senses

Seeing, hearing, smelling, tasting and touching. Grade 1 science.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn what you need to know

Your senses tell you about the world around you. You have five senses. Each sense uses a body part.

👀See with your eyes
👂Hear with your ears
👃Smell with your nose
👅Taste with your tongue
✋Touch with your skin

You often use more than one sense at once. When you eat an orange, you see its colour, smell it, taste it and feel its bumpy skin.

Your senses keep you safe. You hear a car horn, smell smoke or feel a hot stove and move away.

Remember: 5 senses, 5 body parts: eyes, ears, nose, tongue, skin.

Key words

  • senses: the ways your body learns about the world
  • see: use your eyes
  • hear: use your ears
  • touch: feel with your skin

See real-life examples

At the beach

You see the waves, hear the seagulls, smell the salty air, feel the warm sand and taste a cold ice pop.

Fire alarm

A loud beep makes you leave the building. Your ears help keep you safe.

Is it ready?

You smell cookies baking. Your nose tells you they are almost ready.
