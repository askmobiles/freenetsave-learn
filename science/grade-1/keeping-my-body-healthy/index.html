Keeping my body healthy

Healthy food, exercise, sleep and keeping clean. Grade 1 science.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn what you need to know

Your body needs care to stay healthy and strong.

🥦Eat healthy food
💧Drink water
🏃Move and play every day
😴Sleep about 10 hours
🧼Wash your hands
🪥Brush your teeth twice a day

Fruits and vegetables help your body grow. Sweets and chips are treats for once in a while.

Germs are tiny living things that can make you sick. Washing your hands with soap gets rid of them.

Remember: eat well, move, sleep and keep clean.

Key words

  • healthy: strong and well, not sick
  • germs: tiny living things that can make you sick
  • exercise: moving your body to keep it strong

See real-life examples

Before lunch

Priya washes her hands with soap for 20 seconds, about as long as singing "Happy Birthday" twice.

Bedtime

Sleep helps your body grow and your brain learn. A good bedtime helps you feel ready for school.

Snack choice

An apple and some cheese give you lasting energy. Candy gives a quick burst and then you feel tired.
