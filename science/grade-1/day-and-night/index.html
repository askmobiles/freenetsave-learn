Day and night

Learn what you need to know

During the day the Sun is in the sky. It gives us light and warmth.

At night it is dark. We may see the Moon and the stars.

The Earth is always spinning, like a top. When our side of the Earth faces the Sun, it is day. When our side faces away from the Sun, it is night.

The Earth spins around once every day. That is why day and night happen again and again.

Remember: facing the Sun = day. Facing away = night.

Key words

  • day: when our side of the Earth faces the Sun
  • night: when our side of the Earth faces away from the Sun
  • spin: turn around and around

See real-life examples

Sunrise and sunset

In the morning the Sun seems to rise in the east. In the evening it seems to set in the west. Really, the Earth is turning.

Night animals

Owls and bats are awake at night and sleep in the day.

Long summer days

In summer the days are long and it stays light late. In winter it gets dark early.
