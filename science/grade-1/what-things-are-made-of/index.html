What things are made of

Wood, metal, plastic, glass, fabric and more. Grade 1 science.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn what you need to know

Everything is made of materials. A material is the stuff a thing is made from.

🪵wood: tables, pencils
🔩metal: spoons, cars, keys
🧴plastic: bottles, toys
🪟glass: windows, jars
👕fabric: clothes, towels
📄paper: books, boxes

We choose the material for the job. Windows are glass so we can see through them. Pots are metal because metal does not burn. Raincoats are plastic so water runs off.

Some materials come from nature, like wood from trees and wool from sheep. Some are made by people, like plastic.

Remember: pick a material that fits the job.

Key words

  • material: what something is made of
  • natural: comes from nature, like wood or wool
  • waterproof: does not let water through

See real-life examples

A pencil

A pencil has a wood body, a graphite middle, a metal ring and a rubber eraser. Many things use more than one material.

Paper umbrella?

An umbrella made of paper would get soggy in the rain. Umbrellas use waterproof fabric or plastic.

Wool sweater

Wool comes from sheep. It keeps you warm in winter.
