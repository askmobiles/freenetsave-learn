Energy in our lives

Energy makes things move, light up, heat up and make sound. Grade 1 science.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn what you need to know

Energy makes things happen. It makes things move, get warm, light up or make a sound.

☀️The Sun gives light and heat
🍎Food gives your body energy
🔌Electricity runs lamps and TVs
🔋Batteries power toys
⛽Gas makes cars go
💨Wind turns windmills

The Sun is our most important source of energy. It warms the Earth and helps plants grow.

We should save energy. Turn off lights when you leave a room. Close the door to keep heat in.

Remember: energy makes things move, glow, warm up and make sound.

Key words

  • energy: what makes things move, light up or warm up
  • electricity: energy that flows through wires
  • save energy: use less of it

See real-life examples

Your breakfast

Breakfast gives your body energy to run, play and think all morning.

A flashlight

Batteries in a flashlight give energy that makes the bulb light up.

Solar panels

Some roofs have solar panels. They turn sunlight into electricity.
