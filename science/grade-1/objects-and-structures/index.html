Learn what you need to know

A structure is something built to hold up or hold things. Houses, bridges, towers, chairs and tables are structures.

Nature makes structures too: a bird's nest, a beaver lodge, a spider web and a beehive.

🏠house
🌉bridge
🗼tower
🪑chair
🪺nest
🕸️web

Strong structures have a wide, flat base at the bottom. A wide base keeps them from tipping over. Triangles also make structures strong.

Remember: a wide base and triangles make structures strong.

Key words

  • structure: something built to hold up or hold things
  • base: the bottom part that holds the rest up
  • stable: does not tip over easily

See real-life examples

Block tower

Build a tower of blocks. Put the biggest blocks at the bottom. The tower stands taller without falling.

Bridges

Many bridges have triangle shapes in their metal frames. Triangles help them hold heavy cars and trucks.

Bird nest

A bird weaves twigs, grass and mud into a strong cup shape to hold its eggs.
