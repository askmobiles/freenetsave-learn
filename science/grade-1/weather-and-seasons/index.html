Learn what you need to know

Weather is what the air is like outside today. It can be sunny, cloudy, rainy, snowy or windy. It can be hot or cold.

☀️sunny
☁️cloudy
🌧️rainy
❄️snowy
💨windy
⛈️stormy

In Canada we have four seasons. They come in the same order every year.

🌷Spring: warmer, rain, plants grow
🌞Summer: hot and sunny
🍂Fall: cooler, leaves fall
⛄Winter: cold, snow

We choose our clothes for the weather. A coat and boots for snow, a raincoat for rain, a hat and sunscreen for sun.

Remember: spring → summer → fall → winter, then spring again.

Key words

  • weather: what it is like outside today
  • season: a part of the year with its own kind of weather
  • temperature: how hot or cold it is
