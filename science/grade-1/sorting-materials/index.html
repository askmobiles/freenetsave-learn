Learn what you need to know

We can sort things into groups by what they are like. These are called properties.

🪨hard or soft
🧽rough or smooth
🎈heavy or light
🌈colour
🪟see-through or not
🧲sticks to a magnet or not

A rock is hard and rough. A pillow is soft and light. A spoon is smooth and shiny.

We also sort garbage to help the Earth. Paper, plastic, metal cans and glass can be recycled into new things. Food scraps go in the green bin.

Remember: look and feel to sort, and recycle when you can.

Key words

  • sort: put things into groups
  • property: what something is like: hard, soft, smooth, rough
  • recycle: turn used things into new things
