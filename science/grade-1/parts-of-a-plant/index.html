Learn what you need to know

A plant has parts. Each part has a job, just like your body parts.

🌸Flower: makes seeds
🍃Leaves: make food from sunlight
🌿Stem: holds the plant up and carries water
🟤Roots: drink water and hold the plant in the soil
🌰Seeds: grow into new plants

Remember: roots drink, the stem carries, leaves make food, flowers make seeds.

Key words

  • roots: the part under the ground that takes in water
  • stem: the part that holds the plant up
  • leaf: the flat green part that makes food
  • seed: a tiny part that grows into a new plant

See real-life examples

Eating plant parts

You eat plant parts every day. A carrot is a root. Celery is a stem. Lettuce is a leaf. Peas and beans are seeds.

Water travels up

Put a white flower in coloured water. After a day, the petals change colour. The stem carried the water up from the bottom.

Bees and flowers

Bees visit flowers for sweet nectar. They carry yellow pollen from flower to flower. This helps the flowers make seeds.
