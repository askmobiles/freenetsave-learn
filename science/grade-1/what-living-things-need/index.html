What living things need

Food, water, air, space and shelter. Grade 1 science.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn what you need to know

All living things need some things to stay alive and grow.

🍎food
💧water
🌬️air
🏠shelter
📏space to grow
☀️sunlight (plants)

Animals eat plants or other animals for food. Plants make their own food using sunlight, air and water.

Animals need shelter to stay safe and warm. A bird has a nest. A rabbit has a burrow. You have a home.

Remember: food, water, air and shelter keep living things alive.

Key words

  • needs: things you must have to live
  • shelter: a safe place to live
  • wants: nice things you can live without

See real-life examples

A pet fish

A pet fish needs clean water, food flakes and air in its water. If you forget to feed it, it will get sick.

Houseplant

A houseplant needs light from a window, water and soil. In a dark cupboard it would turn yellow.

Needs and wants

You need food, water and a home. You want toys and games. You can live without wants.
