Mixtures and solutions

Pure substances, mechanical mixtures and solutions. Grade 7 science.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn what you need to know

All matter is either a pure substance or a mixture.

⚪Pure substance: only one kind of particle; elements (gold) and compounds (pure water)
🥗Mechanical mixture: you can see the different parts; salad, trail mix, sand and gravel
🧂Solution: looks like one substance, because one part dissolves in another; salt water, tea, air

In a solution, the substance that dissolves is the solute (like sugar). The substance it dissolves in is the solvent (like water). Water is called the universal solvent because it dissolves so many things.

Some mixtures are in between. A suspension has particles that settle if left alone (muddy water, orange juice with pulp). A colloid has particles that stay spread out and never settle (milk, fog).

Remember: solute + solvent = solution. Mechanical mixtures show their parts; solutions do not.

Key words

  • pure substance: matter made of only one kind of particle
  • mechanical mixture: a mixture where the different parts can be seen
  • solute: the substance that dissolves
  • solvent: the substance that does the dissolving

See real-life examples

Tea

When you make tea, hot water (the solvent) dissolves flavour and colour (the solutes) from the leaves.

Italian dressing

Oil-and-vinegar dressing separates when it sits. You shake it before pouring: it is a suspension.

Ocean water

Sea water is a solution of salts in water. It tastes salty but looks clear.
